- **`pin_count_mismatch` / `pin_order_mismatch` / `flat_dut_mismatch`**: all

- **`extraction.parasitic_integrity`**: 4 nets checked, 0 findings (no open
  nets, island components, orphan nodes, suspicious resistors, short
  candidates or capacitance mismatches).

`extraction.model`, quoted from the report (the four fields that make every
delta row explainable):

- **resistance**: "single lumped series resistance per net, distributed as a
  star across that net's device terminals (issue #592) -- not a per-segment,
  distributed RC ladder, *unless* `--distributed-rc` names this net via
  `--critical-net` (issue #977, Epic #709 Phase 2b): then the net's
  terminals are ordered along their approximate physical spread and its
  total R/C is broken into a chain of per-segment resistors (segment length
  proportional to inter-terminal distance) with a ground capacitor at each
  terminal node (proportional to its adjacent segment length/2), instead of
  one star hub -- still an approximation (terminal position is a
  device-placement proxy, not true per-segment routing geometry), but a
  strictly finer-grained one than the single-hub star"
- **capacitance**: "net-to-ground for every net's own (non-coupled)
  area/perimeter, plus net-to-net for the vertical-overlap coupling
  `coupling` describes below -- a coupled net pair gets a direct capacitor
  between their two hub nodes, not just capacitors to the deck's
  ground/substrate net"
- **coupling**: "vertical overlap (crossover) unconditionally -- where one
  net's conductor on an adjacent metal level sits directly over another
  *distinct* net's conductor, that overlap area is charged between the two
  nets instead of to ground (issue #760) -- plus lateral (same-layer,
  sidewall) coupling, but only for a net pair naming one of the caller's
  declared `--critical-net` nets (issue #976): [...] Any same-layer pair
  with neither side named `--critical-net`, and fringe shielding in general,
  are still not modelled"
- **frequency**: "quasi-static -- one frequency-independent R and C per net;
  no skin effect, no distributed transmission-line behavior"

(The report's full `extraction.model` block is the authoritative text; the
ellipses above are where the JSON continues with wording quoted in full
there.)

## Extracted series resistance vs `em_budget.py`

Per-net star-leg resistance in the committed extracted netlist
(`layout/pass_array/pex/20261120-190530-9206133e.extracted.spice`; each leg
is one device terminal's path to the net's pin node):

| net | legs | leg R range (ohm) | sum (ohm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| VOUT | 40 | 2.72 -- 28.59 | 626.2 |
| VIN | 82 | 0.001 -- 17.55 | 768.6 |
| ISNS | 1 | 43.34 | 43.34 |
| PASS_GATE | 41 | 0.001 -- 64.77 | 1418.7 |

`layout/pass_array/em_budget.py`'s hand figure for the array-internal VOUT
path is **14.77 mOhm nominal / 40.54 mOhm max** (contacts + via1 + via2 +
via3 + via4 + distributed metal, each level's cuts/straps combined in
parallel, from the tech LEFs' published R values). The extraction's
current-weighted pin-to-pin addition is **+599 to +643 mOhm** across the 15
corners (r_on column above) -- roughly **40x** the hand figure, and even the
*minimum single leg* on VOUT (2.72 ohm) is ~184x it.

**The disagreement is the extraction model's, not the metal's, and it is a
known one.** The deck's lumped R for a net is the series sum over the net's
whole conductor tree, star-distributed over terminals; a 40-finger comb
strapped on five metal levels is a massively parallel network, so summing
its branches in series overstates the pin-to-terminal resistance by orders
of magnitude. This is 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#5894 (open: "lumped net R sums
tree branches in series"); this cell is now posted there as a reproduction
with the numbers above. Consequences, stated plainly:

- The **absolute** extracted R (and therefore the absolute +600 mOhm, and
  the `vds_mv` delta of +19 to +36 %) is an upper bound several times too
  high; it must not be read as the array's real post-layout dropout
  penalty, and no layout iteration is justified by it.
- The **replica-ratio shift** (40 -> 43.9-48.0) is the same artifact seen at
  the ISNS pin: the single 43.34 ohm leg carries the whole Msense strap tree
  in series, so Msense's drain sits ~50 mV below the Mpass fingers' at its
  ~1.1 mA and its current falls 9-16 %. The physical strap is a 4-row
  parallel stack (`em_budget`-style arithmetic puts it well under an ohm),
  so the physical ratio shift is small; the number recorded here is the
  model's, and is why it moves at all.
- **`em_budget.py` is unaffected** by this finding: its arithmetic combines
  parallel paths explicitly and its published provenance (tech-LEF R values)
  is independent of the extraction deck.

## What this does and does not establish

- The **route is proven end to end on this PDK and cell**: GDS ->
  `klt pex --deck gf180mcu` -> both legs through `klt sim` on the batch
  fleet -> per-corner, per-quantity `delta[]` with a device-only leg
  splitting representation from parasitics. #16's full post-layout re-run
  starts from the invocation above.
- **This report is NOT citable for T1 item 7** (`signoff/block-manifest.json`
  is untouched by this issue): item 7 is about the block, and this is a
  cell-level route proof. See `signoff/README.md`'s item-7 row.
- **The absolute parasitic numbers are the lumped model's**, per #5894; the
  deltas here are upper bounds for R-dominated quantities. The capacitance
  model (net-to-ground + vertical overlap) is inert at these DC operating
  points and is untested by this bench.
- No spec row is changed, and the ratified dropout margin is not re-read
  from these numbers (the schematic-leg r_on is consistent with the
  `sim/dropout-vs-load` records' knee voltages; that bench remains the
  dropout evidence).
