# Record 20260807-105239-64249c6

- **Record ID**: 20260807-105239-64249c6
- **Supersedes**: `20260802-172454-b90b2ba`
- **Claim**: `README.md` target-spec **"Enable / shutdown"** row — *shutdown
  Iq < 3 µA at ff / 125 °C / 3.63 V; disabled output state is pass device
  fully off with no internal active discharge; Vin→Vout leakage ≤ 1 µA at the
  same corner*; plus, on the same runs, the **Iq** row's *< 30 µA at full
  load* in the enabled state. This re-run exists because issue #51 added two
  devices to `design/error_amp.sch` — `Mrza` (a `pfet_03v3` in triode across
  `Rz`, gated by `BG`) and `Rza` (600 kΩ of `ppolyf_u_1k` in series with it),
  `spec/decision-records/DR-0013-adaptive-shelf-from-the-pass-gate.md`. That
  change is the reason this bench is the named risk item: it re-shapes the
  loop's compensation across the whole load axis, and this bench is the only
  one in `sim/` that exercises the loop **large-signal and in the time
  domain**, at 50 mA, through enable.
- **Netlist provenance**: `design/netlist/ldo_core.spice`, frozen at

- **Testbench**: unchanged from the superseded record — `sim/enable-shutdown/`

- **Operating conditions**: unchanged. 50 mA (`Rload` = 36 Ω) for the enabled

## The Iq rows do not move at all, and that is the point

`iq_en_ua`, `iq_off_ua`, `ileak_vin_vout_ua` and `vout_off` are identical to
the superseded record **to every printed digit at all 63 corners**, and
`t_startup_us` agrees to six significant figures (2295.46 vs 2295.47 µs at the
fastest corner, 5771.05 vs 5771.06 µs at the slowest).

That is the prediction DR-0013 makes, measured rather than argued: `Mrza` sits
in the `Rz`/`Cc` branch, which carries no DC current, so it is at `Vds` = 0 in
every DC solution and draws nothing. `Rza` is in series with it and therefore
also carries nothing. The compensation change is free on the ratified Iq row —
which matters, because that row is specified **at full load as well as at no
load**, and 24.81 µA of the 30 µA is already spent.

## What did move: the large-signal transient, by an order of magnitude or two

These are not ratified rows — `README.md` has no line-item for output ringing
or for enable inrush — but they are measured on the same 63 runs, they are the
time-domain shadow of what `sim/loop-stability/` measures in the frequency
domain, and they moved far more than the DC rows did:

| Quantity | superseded (`b90b2ba`) | **this record** | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vout_pp_late`, worst corner | **197.6 mV** (`ss_-40c_2.97v`) | **4.07 nV** (`res_ss_125c_2.97v`) | output peak-to-peak in the settled window, long after startup |
| `vout_pp_late`, best corner | 53.0 mV | 0.569 nV | — |
| `isup_peak_ma`, worst corner | **279.6 mA** (`fs_-40c_3.63v`) | **50.92 mA** (`res_ff_27c_2.97v`) | peak supply current during the enabled transient, at a 50 mA load |
| `vout_max_en`, worst corner | 1.9278 V (+7.1 %) | **1.8120 V** (+0.67 %) | startup overshoot |
| `vout_min_reg`, worst corner | 1.6888 V (−6.2 %) | **1.7989 V** (−0.06 %) | lowest regulated output after settling |
| `clg_min_reg`, worst corner | 2.276 V (`VIN` − 1.02 V) | **2.9699 V** (`VIN` − 0.0001 V) | limit-clamp gate while settled at 50 mA; `VIN` = clamp off |

Read the first row first. On the superseded netlist the output was still
moving by **53–198 mV peak-to-peak** in the settled window at every one of the
63 corners — a steady oscillation, not a decaying transient, since the window
is sampled long after `t_startup`. On this netlist it is **below 5 nV at every
corner**, i.e. numerically zero. The 280 mA supply-current peak on a 50 mA
load is the same phenomenon seen from the supply side, and it is now 50.9 mA,
i.e. the load current and nothing else.

`clg_min_reg` is the tell for *why* the old numbers were what they were: the
limit clamp inside `ldo_ilimit` was being dragged more than a volt below `VIN`
while the part was supposedly settled, which is the clamp responding to the
ringing rather than to a real overload. It now sits within 0.1 mV of `VIN` at
its worst corner — the clamp is simply off, which is the correct state at
50 mA against a 65–80 mA ratified limit.

Two cautions on how far to read this. First, the superseded record's own
`vout_pp_late` numbers were **not** flagged by that run, because the bench has
no bar on that column — so this is not a regression that was missed, it is a
column that was reported and had nothing to compare against. Second, this
bench runs one load (50 mA), one cap (1 µF) and one ESR (100 mΩ), so it says
nothing about the parts of DR-0001's box that
`sim/loop-stability/records/20260807-103351-64249c6.md` still reports as
failing; at 50 mA / 1 µF that record puts the phase margin between 31° and
95° depending on PVT and ESR (209 of its 252 points there pass DR-0001's
45° bar), and the time domain agrees that whatever is left at this bench's own
point is no longer an oscillation.
