`t_startup_us` is unchanged to 4 significant figures at every corner: the
soft-start ramp fix (`BG`-steer, issue #55) is untouched by this issue's
change, exactly as expected — `Cf1`/`Cf2` is a small Miller cap inside the
amplifier's own local loop and does not touch the clamp-arbitration path
`BG`-steer fixed.

**What moved, and why.** `isup_peak_ma`, `vout_pp_late` and the settled
limit-clamp count all shift — concentrated almost entirely at the **coldest
corners** (`ff`/`fs`/`res_ff` at −40 °C gain 30–70 mA of peak startup
current; a handful of 27 °C/2.97 V points fall by 10–20 mA instead). This is
the expected footprint of fixing the resonance the superseded record's own
"What is NOT fixed" section named as issue #53's: `Cf1`/`Cf2` changes the
amplifier's closed-loop response in exactly the frequency band (0.6–0.9 MHz,
per `sim/loop-stability/README.md`) that this bench's own large-signal
hand-over transient rings through, so the transient's shape — not its
existence — moves. It is evidence *for* issue #53's diagnosis (the ring is
the amplifier's own local-loop resonance, not a clamp limit cycle), not a new
defect: the same corners that ring hardest here are exactly the ones
`sim/loop-stability/records/20260802-095235-c828e73.md` reports as its
tightest phase-margin failures (`ss`/`fs`/`sf`/`tt` at −40 °C, 0.33 µF).

**This is not the row this experiment's PASS verdict is about.** The four
ratified rows this bench owns (shutdown Iq, enabled Iq, Vin→Vout leakage,
disabled-output behaviour) are unchanged, per the table above. The startup
overshoot and current-limit-onset rows this transient also touches are owned
by `sim/startup/` and `sim/current-limit/`, checked separately below rather
than read off this bench's own informational window measurements — see
`sim/startup/records/` and `sim/current-limit/records/` for the current
head-of-chain result against this same design state.
