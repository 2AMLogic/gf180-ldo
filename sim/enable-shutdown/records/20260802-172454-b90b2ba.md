# Record 20260802-172454-b90b2ba

- **Record ID**: 20260802-172454-b90b2ba
- **Supersedes**: `20260802-095233-c828e73`
- **Claim**: `README.md` target-spec **"Enable / shutdown"** row — *shutdown
  Iq < 3 µA at ff / 125 °C / 3.63 V; disabled output state is pass device
  fully off with no internal active discharge; Vin→Vout leakage ≤ 1 µA at the
  same corner*; plus, on the same runs, the **Iq** row's *< 30 µA at full
  load* in the enabled state. This re-run exists because issue #53 resized
  five devices in `design/error_amp.sch` to widen the Type-II gain shelf
  (`Cf1`/`Cf2` 12 × 12 µm → 7 × 7 µm, `M2P` 150 µm/2 µm → 60 µm/1 µm, `Mbuf`
  150 µm/1 µm → 60 µm/0.5 µm, `Mbufb` 200 µm/2 µm → 100 µm/1 µm, `MTAIL`
  6 µm/4 µm → 6.6 µm/4 µm —
  `spec/decision-records/DR-0012-shelf-width-is-cc-over-cf.md`). Two of those
  five move device current — `MTAIL` (+10 % input-pair tail) and `Mbufb`
  (+25 % buffer bias) — so unlike the superseded record's change this one
  **can** move the enabled-Iq row, and the large-signal startup transient this
  bench also reports can move as well. This bench is the risk item issue #53's
  own test plan names for any buffer/bias change, so it is re-run in full
  rather than argued about.
- **Netlist provenance**: `design/netlist/ldo_core.spice`, frozen at
  `netlist-snapshots/20260802-172454-b90b2ba.spice` — taken against a **clean

- **Corner matrix run**: 63 points — process {tt, ff, ss, fs, sf, res_ff,
  res_ss} × T {−40, 27, 125 °C} × Vin {2.97, 3.30, 3.63 V}. Full factorial,
  all completed. `bjt_ff` / `bjt_ss` omitted (no bipolar device).
- **Testbench**: unchanged from the superseded record. `sim/enable-shutdown/`
  was not touched by this change, so every number below is directly
  comparable.
- **Operating conditions**: unchanged. 50 mA (`Rload` = 36 Ω) for the enabled
  op point and the transient; load removed (1 GΩ) for the settled disabled op

Every one of the four ratified clauses this bench owns passes at **all 63
corners**, and the run's own flag list reports `none` for each of them
(`shutdown Iq above the ratified 3 uA`, `enabled Iq above the ratified
30 uA`, `Vin->Vout leakage above 1 uA`, `startup failed to reach 1.764 V
inside the enable window`, `disabled output above 10 mV at the end of the
tail`).

**The disabled state does not move at all.** `iq_off_ua`,
`ileak_vin_vout_ua` and `vout_off` are identical to the superseded record to
four-plus significant figures at every one of the 63 corners — expected, since
`EN` low turns the whole bias chain off and the resized devices carry no
current in that state.

**The enabled state moves by exactly the +1.2 µA the change spends.** The
binding ff/125 °C/3.63 V corner goes 23.58 → **24.81 µA** and the best corner
9.59 → 10.06 µA, i.e. the +10 % input-pair tail and +25 % buffer bias land
where DR-0012 says they do. The ratified row is < 30 µA, so the margin at the
binding corner goes from 21 % to **17 %**. The companion amp-level number is
`sim/amp-openloop/records/20260802-163812-deb3dbd.md`: `iq_ua` worst corner
14.98 µA against the amplifier's own 15 µA soft block allocation — that
allocation, not this ratified row, is what now bounds this lever, and DR-0012
records it as such.

## The large-signal transient this bench also reports: improved across the board

These are informational window measurements, not this bench's ratified rows —
startup overshoot is owned by `sim/startup/` and current-limit onset by
`sim/current-limit/`. They are tabulated because the superseded record
tabulated them and because they are where a compensation change shows up.

| Quantity | post-#51 `…-84f67b8` | post-#55 `…-5ea33e8` | superseded `…-c828e73` | **this record** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| enable → 1.764 V (`t_startup_us`) | 27.3 … 2014.7 µs | 2295.5 … 5771.1 µs | 2295.5 … 5771.1 µs | **2295.5 … 5771.1 µs** |
| startup-window maximum (`vout_max_en`) | 1.8421 … 1.9746 V | 1.8419 … 1.9731 V | 1.8479 … 1.9458 V | **1.8303 … 1.9278 V** |
| corners with `vout_max_en` > 1.836 V | 63 / 63 | 63 / 63 | 63 / 63 | **58 / 63** |
| settled output outside ±2 % (`vout_settled`) | — | — | 15 / 63 | **2 / 63** |
| post-settling ripple (`vout_pp_late`) | 0.0806 … 0.2969 V | 0.0903 … 0.2810 V | 0.0947 … 0.3014 V | **0.0530 … 0.1976 V** |
| corners where the limit clamp engages while settled at 50 mA | 54 / 63 | 53 / 63 | 57 / 63 | **39 / 63** |
| how deeply it engages (`clg_min_reg`, min over corners) | 0.091 V | 2.049 V | 1.994 V | **2.276 V** |
| peak supply current, 50 mA startup (`isup_peak_ma`) | 141.1 … 366.0 mA | 128.9 … 202.6 mA | 120.4 … 248.4 mA | **133.7 … 279.6 mA** |

`t_startup_us` is unchanged to within **0.01 µs at every corner** — the
soft-start ramp (issue #55's `BG`-steer) is untouched by this change, as
expected: the resized devices are inside the amplifier's own signal path, not
in the clamp-arbitration path.

**Everything that measures the settled loop improves.** Post-settling ripple
falls by roughly a third at both ends of its range (0.095–0.301 V →
0.053–0.198 V), the number of corners whose settled output leaves the ±2 %
window drops 15 → 2, startup overshoot drops at every corner (worst 1.9458 →
1.9278 V) and clears the +2 % line at 5 corners that previously missed it, and
the settled 50 mA operating point stops pushing `ldo_ilimit`'s clamp at 18
corners that previously engaged it. This is the same effect the loop-stability
matrix reports in the frequency domain
(`sim/loop-stability/records/20260802-171044-db620a6.md`, 1332/4536 vs
1099/4536, and the whole 0.1 mA column now passing): a wider gain shelf leaves
less ringing for the large-signal hand-over to excite.

**The one number that moves the wrong way is `isup_peak_ma`** (248.4 →
279.6 mA worst corner, concentrated at the cold `fs`/`ff`/`res_ff` corners
this quantity has always been worst at). It is not this bench's ratified row —
inrush is `sim/startup/`'s claim, measured there at 4.7 µF rather than at this
bench's 1 µF/50 mA hand-over — and it remains well inside the range this
quantity has occupied since #51 (141–366 mA). It is recorded here because it
is the only informational quantity that got worse, and because it should be
re-read from `sim/startup/records/` rather than from this bench before anyone
draws an inrush conclusion from it.

**Two corners still settle outside ±2 %** (`ss_27c_2.97v` at 1.8377 V and
`sf_-40c_2.97v` at 1.7603 V, against the 1.764–1.836 V window) and 60 of 63
dip below 1.764 V somewhere after settling. Both are pre-existing — 15 and 63
corners respectively in the superseded record — and both are the residual
ripple of a loop that is not yet stable at 50 mA, which is
`sim/loop-stability/`'s open claim (12/756 passing at 50 mA), not a new defect
from this change.
