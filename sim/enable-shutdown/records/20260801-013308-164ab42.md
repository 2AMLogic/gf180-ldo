# Record 20260801-013308-164ab42

- **Record ID**: 20260801-013308-164ab42
- **Claim**: `README.md` target-spec **"Enable / shutdown"** row — *shutdown
  Iq < 3 µA at ff / 125 °C / 3.63 V; disabled output state is pass device
  fully off with no internal active discharge; Vin→Vout leakage ≤ 1 µA at the
  same corner*. Also measured on the same runs, because they are the same
  transient: the **Iq** row's *< 30 µA at full load* in the **enabled** state,
  and — as context for the current-limit record, not as a claim of this
  record — what the limit clamp does during an enable→active transition into
  the full rated load. Issue #11.
- **Netlist provenance**: schematic — `design/netlist/ldo_core.spice`,
  exported by `design/netlist.py` from `design/ldo_core.sch` +
  `design/ldo_ilimit.sch` + `design/error_amp.sch`. Same idealizations as the
  companion current-limit record (`Vref1`, `Fbias`).
- **Corner matrix run**:
  - Process: tt, ff, ss, fs, sf, res_ff, res_ss
  - Temperature: −40 °C, 27 °C, 125 °C
  - Supply: 2.97 V, 3.30 V, 3.63 V (3.3 V ±10 %)
  - 63 points, full factorial, all completed. The row's binding corner
    (ff/125 °C/3.63 V) is in the matrix and is, as predicted, the worst one
    for every leakage-driven quantity here.
  - **Subset reason**: `bjt_ff` / `bjt_ss` omitted — no bipolar device in the
    design.
- **Operating conditions**:
  - Load current: 50 mA (`Rload` = 36 Ω, the full rated load) for the enabled
    op point and for the whole transient; the load is **removed**
    (`Rload` = 1 GΩ) for the settled disabled op point, which is the harder
    case — with no external load, nothing but the 900 kΩ feedback divider
    sinks the disabled-state leakage, so the disabled output floats as high
    as leakage can push it.
  - Output cap: 1.0 µF with 100 mΩ ESR, inside DR-0001's ratified
    0.33–4.7 µF / 0–500 mΩ window.
  - Enable state: **both**, and the transition between them. `EN` = 0 until
    50 µs, `VIN` from 51 µs to 450 µs, 0 thereafter (1 µs edges); the
    settled-state numbers are taken from separate DC operating points at each
    level, the transition numbers from the 700 µs transient.
  - Reference: `Vref1`, an ideal 1.2 V source. Its current does **not** flow
    in `i(vin)`, so it is metered separately and **added into** every
    quiescent number reported here. A real bandgap's own quiescent current is
    in neither number and cannot be until that block exists.
  - `Vin→Vout` leakage is measured with the output **forced to 0 V** while
    disabled, so it is the leakage into a shorted output, not into a floating
    one.
- **Statistical convention**: N/A (corner-matrix claim, not a distribution
  claim). Leakage-driven numbers here carry the models' own temperature and
  corner behaviour but no mismatch sampling.
- **Result**:

  **Shutdown Iq: PASS at all 63 corners.** **0.203 µA** at the binding
  ff/125 °C/3.63 V corner against a ratified **< 3 µA** — a 15× margin — and
  0.06 nA at the coldest/slowest corner. This row was *failing* before this
  issue: with #9's real amplifier in place and no way to gate it, the same
  disabled state drew **9.24 µA**
  (`sim/op-point-sanity/records/20260801-002928-712cb87.md`), because
  `ldo_core`'s `Men` clamp turns the pass device off and nothing turned the
  amplifier off. #11 appended `EN` to `error_amp` and gated the bias inside
  it; `NBIAS` (the amplifier's mirror node) measures **0.000 V** disabled at
  every corner, which is the direct confirmation that the bias branch is not
  merely small but open.

  **Vin→Vout leakage: PASS at all 63 corners.** **0.214 µA** at
  ff/125 °C/3.63 V against a ratified **≤ 1 µA**. This is dominated by the
  2 mm pass device's own off-state leakage, so it will scale with the pass
  device when it is re-sized to the ratified ~4 mm — at 4 mm it would be
  ≈ 0.43 µA, still inside the row, and that is the number a later sizing
  issue should re-check rather than assume.

  **Disabled output state: PASS at all 63 corners, with one number worth
  reading carefully.** The pass-device gate sits at `VIN` (to within the
  print resolution) at every corner, and **nothing in the design discharges
  the output** — which is exactly what the row asks for. The consequence,
  visible in the no-load disabled op point, is that the output *floats up on
  leakage* until the 900 kΩ divider sinks it: **0.180 V** at ff/125 °C/3.63 V
  (0.18 V / 900 kΩ ≈ 0.2 µA, the same leakage as above, so the two numbers
  are consistent), and ≈ 20 µV at the coldest corner. With any real external
  load present it collapses to millivolts (2.3 mV measured at the end of the
  disabled tail of the transient, with the 36 Ω load attached). A consumer
  that needs a hard-zero disabled output needs an explicit discharge device,
  which this row deliberately does not ask for.

  **Enabled Iq at full rated load: PASS at all 63 corners.** **22.38 µA** at
  ff/125 °C/3.63 V — the Iq row's own binding corner — against a ratified
  **< 30 µA**, i.e. 25 % margin *including* the current-limit block's
  ≈ 3.6 µA. 9.44 µA at ss/−40 °C/2.97 V.

  **Enable → active transition (reported, not claimed).** The regulator
  reaches the −2 % edge of the accuracy window **27.9–63.7 µs** after `EN`
  rises at every corner, into the full 50 mA load, with no latch-low failure
  anywhere — which is the behavioural point a *brickwall* limit buys and a
  folded-back one could have cost. Two honest caveats, both out of this
  issue's stated acceptance bar (which covers the settled enabled and
  disabled states, not the glitch during the transition itself) and both
  belonging to the **Startup** row, which no issue has yet claimed:

  - **Overshoot exceeds the Startup row's +2 %** at 62 of 63 corners (worst
    1.917 V, +6.5 %, at res_ff/−40 °C/2.97 V). There is no soft-start in this
    design.
  - **Inrush is 153–289 mA**, not the Startup row's ≤ 5 mA, for the same
    reason: with no controlled ramp the output capacitor is charged as fast
    as the pass device and the limit loop's bandwidth allow. The DC limit is
    62–94 mA (companion record); the limit loop is deliberately slowed by
    `Cc` so it cannot fight the main loop, so it does not catch a
    microsecond-scale capacitor-charging edge. A soft-start ramp is the fix
    and it is a separate design element, not a re-tune of this one.

  **Does the limit engage inside the rated load, transiently?** The clamp's
  gate (`CLG`) rests at `VIN` when the limit is idle, so its minimum over the
  settled window is a direct waveform-level answer. At **59 of 63 corners it
  never moves** (≥ `VIN` − 0.2 V). At the four exceptions — all ff, all with
  `res_ff` resistors — it does dip, and the same run says why: those corners
  are **ringing**, with 110–152 mV of peak-to-peak output ripple in the
  settled window and a peak supply current of **110–135 mA** against a 50 mA
  DC load. The pass device's current is the load current *plus* whatever the
  1 µF output cap is being charged with, so a marginally-stable loop presents
  the limit with real >100 mA current spikes and the limit correctly clamps
  them. That is the loop's phase margin (issue #10's claim), not a limit that
  trips at 50 mA of load: at the 24 corners that do not ring (≤ 5 mV ripple)
  the peak settled current is ≈ 50 mA and `CLG` never moves. The DC evidence
  in the companion record is unambiguous on the row's actual words — onset is
  ≥ 62 mA at every corner.

  **Overall: PASS** — every sub-claim of the Enable/shutdown row passes at
  every corner, and the Iq row passes at its binding corner. The startup
  overshoot/inrush observations above are recorded as findings against a row
  this record does not claim.
