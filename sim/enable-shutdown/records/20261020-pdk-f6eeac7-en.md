## Old vs new

There is **no same-DUT baseline** for this bench: the newest earlier record,
`20260905-232555-3093ea1`, measured an older `ldo_core`, so a difference
against it mixes the netlist change with the PDK-revision change and is not
attributable to either. No cross-record delta is claimed. The same-DUT,
same-bench evidence that the re-pin is neutral for this netlist is
`20261020-pdk-f6eeac7-iq` vs `20261015-iq-320-pvt` and
`20261020-pdk-f6eeac7-ilim` vs `20261015-ilim-320-pvt` (both identical to the
printed precision), which share the enabled/disabled operating points used here.
