### What is fixed: the soft-start ramp is no longer bypassed

`t_startup_us` is back in the pre-#51 band **at every one of the 63 corners**
(2295.5 … 5771.1 µs against 2288.9 … 5764.1 µs; the largest per-corner
deviation from the pre-#51 value is under 0.5 %). That is the direct
measurement of the thing #55 is about: the clamps can hold `PASS_GATE` again,
so `VOUT` tracks the soft-start ramp instead of the main loop's own slew.
Peak supply current during startup falls from 141–366 mA to 129–203 mA, and
the depth to which the limit clamp is driven falls from `CLG` = 0.091 V — the
clamp saturated, fully on, and still losing the node — to 2.049 V, i.e. it is
now regulating rather than railed.

An exploratory single-corner waveform capture (tt/27 °C/2.97 V, `NO_RECORD`,
not evidence) makes the mechanism visible: with the fix, `VOUT` rises linearly
from 0 V at 0.2 ms to 1.77 V at 3.8 ms with supply current ramping 5 → 50 mA,
tracking 1.5 × `SSR` the whole way. On the unmodified post-#51 netlist at the
same corner `VOUT` is already at 1.807 V by 0.3 ms with `SSR` still at
0.064 V — the ramp is not being tracked at all.

### What is NOT fixed, and why it is not this issue's

`vout_max_en` and `clg_min_reg` barely move. Both are **window** measurements
that now catch a *settled-state* limit cycle rather than anything about
startup:

- `vout_max_en` is `MAX v(vout)` over 101 µs … 8 ms, i.e. the whole enable
  window including the settled region. At tt/27 °C/2.97 V its maximum occurs
  at **5.73 ms**, two milliseconds after the ramp finished at 3.69 ms.
- `clg_min_reg` and `isup_peak_reg_ma` are taken over 7 … 8 ms — settled,
  50 mA, well after hand-over.

The dedicated experiments that own those two ratified rows both pass and both
match their pre-#51 records:

- `sim/startup/records/20260802-032332-5ea33e8.md` measures startup overshoot
  against the final value and reports **negative** overshoot (−34.8 …
  −27.0 mV) at **45/45** points — no overshoot at all — against −32.4 …
  −23.3 mV pre-#51.
- `sim/current-limit/records/20260802-023438-5ea33e8.md` measures the limit
  onset by DC V-I sweep and reports **"corners where the limit engages at or
  below 50 mA: none"**, threshold 62.01 … 93.83 mA, bit-identical to the
  pre-#55 record.

**The residual is the amplifier's own output stage, not the clamps.** Measured
directly, at tt/27 °C/2.97 V, on the **unmodified post-#51 netlist** with
`Mclamp_ss` *and* `Mclamp` both disconnected from `PASS_GATE` — i.e. with no
clamp able to touch the node at all (exploratory, `NO_RECORD`, not evidence):

| variant (tt/27 °C/2.97 V) | `vout_pp_late` | `isup_peak_reg_ma` | `vout_max_en` |
|---|---|---|---|
| pre-#51 | 0.0000 V | — | 1.8137 V |
| post-#51, unmodified | 0.1853 V | 176.7 mA | 1.9093 V |
| post-#51, **both clamps disconnected** | **0.2949 V** | **326.3 mA** | **2.0653 V** |
| this record's design | 0.1775 V | 148.6 mA | 1.8954 V |

Removing the clamps entirely makes the settled ring *worse*, so the ring is
not a clamp limit cycle. Three further exploratory probes agree: shrinking
`Cm_ss` 7.2 pF → 0.8 pF (ring worse: `vout_pp_late` 0.212), re-referencing it
to `VSS` (worse: 0.337), and disabling `ldo_ilimit`'s `BG` device (unchanged)
all leave it in place. In the waveform the clamp gate `CLG` is a *passive
follower* of `PASS_GATE` through `Cm_ss` during the ring, while `N1` and `BG`
slew rail-to-rail at ~250 kHz — the amplifier is driving, the clamp is being
driven.

That resonance is issue **#53**'s (`fix(error_amp): damp the gate buffer's
follower resonance`) and is visible in this repo's own loop-stability evidence
as the 0.6 … 0.9 MHz feature discussed under `sim/loop-stability/`. It is not
in #55's scope and is not attempted here.
