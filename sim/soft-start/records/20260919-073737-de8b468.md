## 3. Why the element's own bias now switches off, and what the measurement shows

`design/ldo_softstart.sch` ("HOW THE BRANCH SUPPLY IS GATED AFTER
HAND-OVER") and DR-0028 give the mechanism in full. The one-line version:
`Mgmg_ss` sits between `VIN` and the branches' new shared supply node
`VING`, its gate is the mirror's own reference node `GMRM`, and when
DR-0023's rectification event puts the `Mgmr_ss`/`Mgmrc_ss` stack into
cutoff, `GMRM` rises toward `VIN` and takes `Mgmg_ss`'s `Vsg` with it.
`Rgmg_ss` (450 kΩ) bleeds across it so the branches are throttled rather
than starved, which is what keeps `GMIA`/`GMRM`/`GMSUM` determined — DR-0027
measured what happens without it (no physical DC solution at
`ss_-40c_2.97v`).

The Iq consequence is the companion record's, not this one's, but the two
numbers this bench can see are consistent with it:

- At the `sink`/`ssr=1.15` state (`tt_27c_3.30v`, §4's operating point) the
  element's injection falls from **+0.3935 µA** to **+0.2927 µA** and the
  landed `VOUT` rises from 1.68129 V to 1.71152 V — less residual injection
  pushing `FB` up, i.e. T1's improvement seen from the loop-gain deck rather
  than the hand-over deck.
- `hg_end` and `ssr_end` are unchanged to the digit (2.9698…3.6336 V and
  1.9871…2.5227 V), so the hold/release sequencing this cell depends on is
  not perturbed.
