## Result: PASS on the ramp itself, FAIL on the settling window and on two transients

**The controlled-ramp clause passes at all 163 points.** The steady ramp rate,
taken as 1.0 V / (t(1.4 V) − t(0.4 V)), is **0.296 … 0.867 V/ms** against the
ratified ≤ 1 V/ms — 13% margin at the fastest corner (ff/125 °C/2.97 V), and
independent of load and of C_eff to within a percent, which is the property a
reference-tracking soft start is supposed to have and a current-limited one is
not.

**At 0.33 µF with 500 mΩ of ESR — the smallest capacitor in the ratified

### Where the failures come from, and they are two different things

**1. The settling window (15 / 63) is the ramp rate's PVT spread, not a
transient.** The ramp is `V_REF / (R_ss_bias · C_ss)`, an absolute on-chip
`R × C`. Measured spread over the 163 points is **2.93 : 1** (0.296 to
0.867 V/ms), which is what the PDK's own corner cards predict: `ppolyf_u_3k`
is 3000 ± 750 Ω/□ (±25%) and `cap_mim_2f0` is ±10%, and the `ff` / `ss`
corners move both in the same direction, giving 2.04 : 1 from process before
the poly resistor's temperature coefficient widens it further. The two clauses
"≤ 1 V/ms" and "±2% within 3 ms" jointly admit only a **1.70 : 1** window
(a rising ramp enters the ±2% band at 1.764 V, which is what `m_t_startup`
measures, so a 1 V/ms ramp needs 1.764 ms to get there and the admissible band
is 0.588–1.0 V/ms). 2.93 does not fit inside 1.70, and no re-centring of this
design or any other untrimmed on-chip ramp changes that. This is DR-0004's own
pre-registered revisit trigger for this row, and it is the subject of
`spec/decision-records/DR-0006`.

**2. The inrush, overshoot and peak-supply failures are two short transients
per startup, not the ramp.** `dvout_max` (the largest instantaneous dV_out/dt
anywhere in the enable window) runs 4–10× the steady ramp rate at the same
point, and the excess is concentrated in (a) the soft-start loop acquiring
control at the bottom of the ramp, ~200–450 µs after the enable edge, and (b)
the hand-over to the main loop at the top, when `error_amp` — which has been
saturated on the more-current side for the whole ramp, because its input error
is `V_REF − FB` and that is far outside its ±5 mV linear range until the very
end — comes out of saturation. Multiply the excess by C_eff and it is exactly
the measured inrush: 4.7 µF × 4.8 V/ms = 22.6 mA, against a measured 22.3 mA
at 4.7 µF / 500 mΩ. The *ramp's* own inrush at the top of the capacitor window
is 4.7 µF × 0.836 V/ms = **3.9 mA**, inside the ratified 5 mA.

### What the ESR axis says: the low-ESR failures are pre-existing

Every group with 500 mΩ of ESR passes the overshoot clause at every corner.
Every group with 1 mΩ fails it somewhere, and 0.33 µF / 1 mΩ fails it
everywhere, with `vout_pp_late` (peak-to-peak output ripple in a 400 µs window
7.5 ms after enable, i.e. long after any startup transient) up to **0.962 V** —
that is a sustained oscillation, not a startup artifact.

That corner is not created by this issue. The **pre-#38** enable/shutdown
record measures `vout_pp_late` of up to **0.179 V** at `res_ff_125c_2.97v` in
its own settled window at 1 µF, and the re-run of that same bench against the
post-#38 design measures 0.216 V at the same corner
(`sim/enable-shutdown/records/20260801-071016-6026a64.md`) — i.e. the ff /
res_ff family was already ringing in regulation before the soft start existed.
DR-0001 and DR-0004 both name the 0.33 µF end of the capacitor window as the
hardest point in the design, and main-loop compensation is issue #10's subject,
not this one's. **This record does not claim to have fixed it, and does not
claim the soft start is stable there.**

## Bench notes and caveats, stated rather than buried

- **`m_dvout_min` is a whole-window metric.** It is negative at every point
  because the window (101 µs → 8 ms) also contains post-hand-over settling
  ripple, so it does **not** by itself falsify the monotonicity clause. The
  clean monotonicity evidence is the 0.33 µF / 500 mΩ and 4.7 µF / 500 mΩ
  groups, where `vout_max_en` is within 11 mV of the settled value at all 40
  points — a ramp that overshoots its endpoint by 11 mV cannot have dipped on
  the way up.
- **`m_vout_off_tran` above 10 mV at the 0 mA points is a bench artifact**, not
  a design result. The disabled tail is 1 ms and the only discharge path at
  0 mA load is the 900 kΩ feedback divider, so a 1 µF output falls with a
  0.9 s time constant. The settled disabled state is measured properly, as a
  DC operating point with the load removed, in `sim/enable-shutdown`.
- **The hand-over invariant on `CLG` is met at 147 of 163 points, not all
  163.** `tb_soft_start.spice.in` states the invariant — "`SSR` must finish
  ABOVE `VREF` (1.2 V) and `CLG` must finish at `VIN`, or the soft-start clamp
  never disengaged and is still fighting the main loop" — and measures both
  ends of it, but only the `SSR` half was adjudicated in `summarize.py` when
  this run was made. The `CLG` half is now adjudicated too (predicate
  `m_clg_end` more than 0.2 V below `VIN`; 0.2 V is a reporting threshold, not
  a ratified bound, chosen well under the |Vtp| range in
  `sim/devchar/CONCLUSIONS.md`). Re-run against these same committed logs it
  names **16 points**, 11 of them by more than 0.3 V, worst
  `tt_-40c_2.97v_0.33u_0.001_36` at `CLG` = 2.1213 V against `VIN` = 2.97 V
  (Vsg = 0.85 V). **All sixteen are points that are still ringing at 7.9 ms**:
  `vout_pp_late` runs 0.034–0.962 V across them, every one of them above the
  18 mV "still ringing" threshold, and twelve are at the 1 mΩ ESR edge of
  DR-0001's window. So at none of the sixteen is `clg_end` a settled value —
  it is one sample of a node that is still moving, which is the same
  pre-existing low-ESR oscillation the ESR section above attributes to issue
  #10. The four at the nominal 1 µF / 100 mΩ output
  (`ff_125c_2.97v_1u_0.1_36`, `res_ff_125c_2.97v_1u_0.1_36`,
  `ff_27c_3.30v_1u_0.1_36`, `res_ff_125c_3.30v_1u_0.1_36`, Vsg 0.21–0.50 V,
  `vout_pp_late` 0.10–0.21 V) are all in the ff / res_ff family. This is
  nonetheless the honest exception to `design/README.md`'s statement that this
  block's steady-state footprint on `PASS_GATE` is a device in cutoff, and
  that statement has been amended rather than left standing. **The CSV is
  unchanged**: the predicate is
  a report-time verdict on the same measurement (`clg_end`) this record has
  carried from the start, and no logs were re-simulated to add it. What this
  does not license: any claim that the residual `Vsg` is or is not affecting
  the main loop. That is an AC question and issue #10 owns it; what is
  measured here is the transient endpoint only.
- **`m_isup_peak_ma` is compared against 52 mA**, i.e. 10 mA below the
  **measured** worst-corner current limit of 62.0 mA
  (`sim/current-limit/records/20260801-012857-164ab42.md`), not below the
  ratified 65 mA floor — DR-0005 already records why the measured floor is
  where it is.
- The ratified inrush number is quoted "at C_eff = 4.7 µF", and this record
  reports both the ramp's own inrush at that capacitor (3.9 mA, passing) and
  the measured peak including the two transients (12.7–22.3 mA at 500 mΩ,
  failing). Both are stated because the row does not distinguish them and this
  record is not entitled to decide that on its own.

## What this record does and does not license

- It licenses the claim that the design now has a **controlled output ramp
  whose rate is a design parameter**, bounded below 1 V/ms at every corner and
  every capacitor in DR-0001's window, and that startup peak supply current is
  down by a factor of 2–3 at every corner.
- It does **not** license the claim that the ratified Startup row passes. It
  does not, on four of its clauses, and this record is the evidence for that.
  `spec/decision-records/DR-0006` proposes the one amendment the physics
  forces (the settling window) and explicitly declines to propose the others.
