## What changed in the circuit, and why it is not "ramp the amplifier's reference"

`#38`'s schematic notes record, with a measurement, why the obvious soft start
does not work on this amplifier: `error_amp` has an **NMOS** input pair, so
feeding its reference from a ramp leaves both inputs under the pair's
common-mode floor while `VOUT` is near 0, the first stage has no gain, and the
loop is not closed over the bottom of the ramp. That is why `#38` built a
second loop instead.

Injection into `FB` is a different circuit with a different failure mode,
because it keeps **both** amplifier inputs at `VREF` for the whole ramp:

    At node FB, with I_inj sourced in,  (VOUT − FB)/Rtop + I_inj = FB/Rbot.
    The main loop drives FB to VREF, so   VOUT = 1.5·VREF − I_inj·Rtop.
    With  I_inj = (VREF − SSR)/(Rtop‖Rbot) = (VREF − SSR)/200 kΩ,
          VOUT = 1.5·SSR,  and I_inj = 0 exactly when SSR = VREF.

So `FB` sits at 1.2 V — comfortably inside the NMOS pair's common-mode range —
from the first microsecond, and the amplifier that regulates the ramp is the
**main** one, already compensated for every output network in DR-0001's
window. There is no second loop to acquire, so there is no acquisition
transient to damp. Hand-over needs no comparator, switch or timer: `SSR`
finishes above `VREF` (`Mtop_ss`'s ceiling, measured 1.527–2.425 V here), the
injection is source-only, so it rectifies hard off and contributes exactly
zero DC error afterwards — `vout_settled` is inside ±2 % at **163/163** points
(it was 161/163).

## The part that is not free: two enable-edge transients, measured

Pinning `FB` is necessary and **not sufficient**. Both of the following were
measured on this bench at `ff/125 °C/2.97 V, 4.7 µF/500 mΩ, 50 mA` (the
superseded record's worst in-scope point, 14.86 mA) on scratch builds that are
not committed, and each is reproducible by deleting the named devices from
`design/ldo_softstart.sch`:

| build | `icap_peak` | what it shows |
|---|---|---|
| injection alone (no pre-charge, no hold, no ramp delay) | **238 mA** @ 4 µs after the edge | `Cff` (15 pF) plus the input pair's gate capacitance means 6 µA needs several µs to lift `FB` to 1.2 V, while `error_amp`'s bias is up ~0.5 µs after the edge. For those few µs the amplifier correctly sees `FB ≪ VREF` and turns the pass device fully on. |
| injection + `FB` pre-charge + ramp delay, **no hold** | **240 mA** @ 3 µs | Even with `FB` pinned from t = 0, `error_amp` wakes with `N1`/`ND` parked at `VDD` (`Mn1_pu`/`Mnd_pu`), so `M2P` is off, `M2N` pulls `BG` down and `#51`'s follower `Mbuf` slams `PASS_GATE` down before the first stage has settled. This one is independent of `FB` entirely. |
| injection + hold + ramp delay, **no pre-charge**, τ ≈ 3 µs | 105 mA | hold releases before `FB` is up |
| all three, τ = 15 µs | 23 mA | hold releases before `error_amp` has settled |
| all three, τ = 50 µs | 4.87 mA | inside the bound, no margin |
| **all three, τ = 147 µs — as built** | **3.77 mA** | also drops peak `dVout/dt` to 0.80 V/ms |

The last row is the only one that is also a committed measurement: this
record's own `ff_125c_2.97v_4.7u_0.5_36` log reads **3.77019 mA**. The τ = 147 µs
scratch build used `Rh_ss`/`Rr_ss` = 2000 squares with 109 µm × 109 µm
capacitors and read 3.77025 mA; the as-built cell reaches the same τ with
4870 squares and 70 µm × 70 µm capacitors, which is the minimum-area R/C split
for that τ (equal resistor and capacitor area) and, as the two numbers show,
electrically the same delay.

The three elements that fix them (`Mpre_a_ss`/`Mpre_b_ss`, `Mhold_ss`/
`Mhold_bg_ss`, and the two RCs that sequence them) are described in
`design/ldo_softstart.sch`'s own notes. Two properties are worth restating
here because they are what makes the fix a design rather than a tuning:

- **The `FB` pre-charge switch carries zero current at its own operating
  point.** With `VOUT` = 0 and `SSR` = 0 the injection's 6 µA is *exactly* the
  divider's draw at `FB` = `VREF`, so the switch supplies only the transient
  charge into `Cff` and opens without a step. That is a property of the
  algebra above, not of a tuned value.
- **The two RCs are deliberately the same R and the same C** (`ppolyf_u_3k`
  4870 squares, `cap_mim` 70 µm × 70 µm). The design depends on their
  *ordering* — the hold must release (at ≈1.3 τ) before the ramp starts (at
  ≈1.5 τ) — and matched devices keep that ordering at every corner even
  though τ itself moves ±25 % with the resistor corner. The absolute delay is
  not load-bearing; the order is.

## What is idealized, and what that costs this record

`Binj_ss` is a **behavioural source**, not a circuit:

    I(VIN -> FB) = max(0, (VREF - SSR) * 5.0e-6) * (EN > 1.4 V)

— a 5 µA/V linear transconductor, a source-only rectifier and an enable gate
in one element. Issue #189 explicitly permits this ("or an idealized VCCS if
this design's existing precedent for idealized soft-start elements —
`Fss_ramp`, `Fbias` — extends to this"), and this cell already carries
`Fss_ramp` on the same footing. It is nonetheless a **larger** idealization
than `Fss_ramp` (an ideal large-ratio mirror), and this record therefore does
**not** substantiate:

- the transconductor's own PVT spread. The intended implementation is two
  `VIN`-referenced resistor-degenerated PMOS branches with `SSR` and `VREF`
  on their gates, differenced in a diode-connected PMOS — which *is* the
  rectifier, since it simply turns off when `SSR` passes `VREF` — and
  mirrored into `FB`. Its dominant error is the two branches' unequal
  currents making their |Vgs| unequal: ~3 % of the injection at `SSR` = 0,
  falling to exactly zero at hand-over (where the branch currents are equal
  by construction). 3 % of the injection is ~50 mV of `VOUT` at the *start*
  of the ramp, where the ratified clauses have their largest margins.
- the quiescent current it adds. The settled `Iq` row has ~8 µA of headroom
  at its binding corner (22.0 µA measured against < 30 µA,
  `sim/quiescent-current/records/20260905-200855-3093ea1.md`); the branch
  currents scale as 1/R and a 2 MΩ-class reference resistor keeps the adder
  under ~2 µA, but that is arithmetic, not a measurement.
- its area.

**The one property the device-level build must preserve is that the 5.0e-6
coefficient is `1.5/Rtop` — a RATIO to the feedback divider, not an absolute
transconductance.** If the injection's reference resistor does not track
`Rtop`, the `VOUT`/`SSR` gain moves with the resistor corner, and at −25 % the
ramp would start at +0.45 V instead of 0 — which is a step into `C_out`, i.e.
exactly the failure this issue removes. `ldo_core`'s divider is itself two
ideal resistors in this design, so expressing the injection as a ratio to it
is consistent with how the divider ratio is already treated (README.md note
3), but it is an assumption and it is stated here as one.

Issue **#191** tracks replacing `Binj_ss` with that device-level
transconductor and re-running this bench (and `sim/quiescent-current`) against
it.

  ```
  set wnflag=1

  ```

  - **This refines the superseded record's `JOBS=1` note.** That record
    observed that `run.sh`'s default `JOBS=8` turns a ~15 s point into a
    >30 min point on this 8-core host, and worked around it by serialising the
    sweep. The mechanism is oversubscription, not the parallel fan-out:
    this `ngspice-46` build is OpenMP-threaded and takes ~6 threads *per
    process* by default, so `JOBS` × 6 threads compete for 8 cores — and on a
    shared host they also compete with whatever else is running. Measured on
    one 9 ms point (`tt_-40c_2.97v_1u_0.1_36`, this design) **on an idle
    host**, threading itself is worth almost nothing: **14.6 s** wall with
    default threading against **12.8 s** with `num_threads=1`. Under
    concurrency the difference is the whole ball game: with default threading
    and `JOBS=2` on a co-tenanted host, two points had not finished after
    16 minutes; with `num_threads=1` the entire 163-point matrix completes in
    **6 minutes** at `JOBS=4`. Pinning the thread count is what makes `JOBS`
    mean what it says.
  - `num_threads` is an ngspice variable, not an environment variable:
    `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` does **not** work (measured — ngspice overrides it
    from its own default), and a local `.spiceinit` **replaces** `~/.spiceinit`
    rather than adding to it, which is why the `set wnflag=1` line above must
    be repeated in it. Omitting it makes every PDK model lookup fail with
    "could not find a valid modelname".
  - **Numerically identical, verified rather than assumed**, twice: (a) the
    *superseded* record's netlist re-run under `num_threads=1` at
    `tt_27c_3.30v_1u_0.1_36` reproduces that record's committed
    `icap_peak_ma` of **2.79448 mA** exactly; (b) this design's
    `tt_-40c_2.97v_1u_0.1_36` gives the same `4.86621e-01 mA at 4.41839e-04 s`
    under both thread settings, to every printed digit.
  - This is a harness-usage observation, not a finding about the design, and
    it is left as a note here rather than as a change to `run.sh` (which would
