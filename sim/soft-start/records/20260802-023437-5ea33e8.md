## Result: the ramp clause is untouched; the post-ramp transients are #51's

### The clause this issue is about: **PASS, 163/163, and unmoved**

| Quantity | pre-#51 `…-6026a64` | **this record** | ratified |
|---|---|---|---|
| steady ramp rate, all 163 points | 0.296 … 0.867 V/ms | **0.316 … 0.836 V/ms** | ≤ 1 V/ms — **163/163** |
| enable → 1.764 V, main 63 | 2.2889 … 5.7641 ms | **2.2955 … 5.7710 ms** | — |
| ramp-rate points above 1 V/ms | 0 / 163 | **0 / 163** | — |
| points failing to reach 1.764 V inside the window | 0 / 163 | **0 / 163** | — |
| points where the ramp did not finish above `VREF` | 0 / 163 | **0 / 163** | — |

The ramp rate and the time to regulation reproduce the pre-#51 record to
**within 0.15 % at every one of the 163 points**. That is the measurement #55
exists to produce: the clamp can hold `PASS_GATE` again, so `VOUT` is once more
governed by `1.5 × SSR` rather than by the main loop's own slew. On the
unmodified post-#51 design the same quantity on the companion bench collapsed
to 27 … 2015 µs (`sim/enable-shutdown/records/20260801-200827-84f67b8.md`) —
the ramp was not being tracked at all.

### The `t_startup > 3 ms` failure: unchanged, and already spec'd

48/63 on the main matrix and 118/163 overall, the same points and the same
values as the superseded record. This is the ramp's absolute on-chip `R × C`
spread (2.64 : 1 measured here) against a joint constraint that admits only
1.70 : 1 — `spec/decision-records/DR-0006`. #55 does not move it in either
direction.

### The inrush / overshoot / peak-supply failures: worse, and not the clamp's

Main 63-point matrix, C_eff = 1 µF / 100 mΩ, 50 mA:

| Quantity | pre-#51 | **this record** | ratified | points passing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| steady ramp rate | 0.316 … 0.836 V/ms | **0.316 … 0.836 V/ms** | ≤ 1 V/ms | **63 / 63** |
| enable → 1.764 V | 2.29 … 5.76 ms | **2.30 … 5.77 ms** | ≤ 3 ms | 15 / 63 |
| overshoot (`vout_max_en`) | 1.8034 … 1.9086 V | **1.8409 … 1.9342 V** | ≤ 1.836 V | 0 / 63 |
| inrush (`icap_peak_ma`) | 3.59 … 86.9 mA | **79.5 … 148.8 mA** | ≤ 5 mA | 0 / 63 |
| peak supply current | 53.2 … 137.2 mA | **128.7 … 199.5 mA** | ≤ 52 mA | 0 / 63 |
| settled ripple (`vout_pp_late`) | 0.0000 … 0.2147 V | **0.0879 … 0.2622 V** | — | — |

**These are window maxima taken over the whole 8 ms enable window, and what
they are catching is a settled-state limit cycle that starts after the ramp
finishes, not the ramp.** The attribution is measured, not asserted, and lives
in `sim/enable-shutdown/records/20260802-023434-5ea33e8.md`: at
tt/27 °C/2.97 V, on the **unmodified post-#51 netlist with both clamps
disconnected from `PASS_GATE` entirely**, `vout_pp_late` is 0.295 V and peak
settled supply current is 326 mA — *worse* than with the clamps present. A
clamp cannot be the cause of a ring that gets worse when the clamp is removed.
Three further probes (shrinking `Cm_ss` to 0.8 pF, re-referencing it to `VSS`,
removing `ldo_ilimit`'s `BG` device) leave it in place or worsen it.

The missing middle column is worth naming: there is **no post-#51, pre-#55
soft-start record**, so this bench cannot separate #51 from #55 on its own. The
companion enable/shutdown bench can, because it has all three at the identical
1 µF / 100 mΩ / 50 mA operating point with the identical stimulus — peak supply
current there is 53.2 … 138.9 mA pre-#51, **141.1 … 366.0 mA post-#51**, and
128.9 … 202.6 mA with this fix. This record's 128.7 … 199.5 mA sits on that
last column.

The **0 mA** row is the informative one, because it is the only group in the
matrix where the settled-state ring is absent: there this design **improves**
on pre-#51 — overshoot passes 20/20 against 16/20 (1.8030–1.8103 V against
1.8016–1.8442 V) and inrush 3.90–7.63 mA against 4.04–8.59 mA. Every group that
is *loaded* is dominated by the ring.

Two worst-case numbers across all 163 points also improve against pre-#51:
peak overshoot **2.327 V → 2.095 V** and worst settled ripple
**0.962 V → 0.659 V**, both at the 0.33 µF / 1 mΩ corner DR-0001 and DR-0004
already name as the hardest point in the design.

**Overall: PASS on the ramp clause at 163/163 and on the 0 mA capacitor point;
FAIL on settling (DR-0006, unchanged) and on inrush / overshoot / peak supply
at every loaded point — the last three dominated by a ~250 kHz settled-state
limit cycle in `error_amp`'s output stage that is issue #53's, is present with
the clamps removed, and is not created or worsened by the clamp change this
record was minted for.**
