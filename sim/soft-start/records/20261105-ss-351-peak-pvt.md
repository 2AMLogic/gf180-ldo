## Verdict

**The servo does not change the startup peak anywhere in the tested matrix.**
Across the 163 points per DUT (326 results, all `pass`, none missing or
non-finite) the two DUTs agree, wherever the peak is a real hold-release
event (161 of 163 points, peak Icap > 50 mA), to:

- peak `Icap`: <= 0.20 %; peak supply current: <= 0.19 %;
- peak `dV/dt`: <= 0.22 %;
- time of the peak: <= 0.004 us.

So the 142 vs 282 mA (99 vs 369 V/ms) difference of
`20261007-ss-320-tt27-probe` stays withdrawn as a `tran 1u 9m` sampling
artifact, now at every tested condition, not only at tt/27 C/3.30 V.
**DR-0037 must not cite a lower inrush or peak `dV/dt` from the servo.**

## Results

Peak sizes from the 5 ns step run (values in `summary.csv`):

| set | peak Icap (mA) | peak dV/dt (V/ms) | time of peak (us) |
|---|---|---|---|
| main config, 63 points | 91.7 .. 416.6 | 103.7 .. 549.0 | 135.4 .. 172.6 |
| all 161 event points | 66.2 .. 456.9 | up to 2104.7 (res_ff, -40 C, 2.97 V, 0.33 uF / 0.5 ohm) | 135.3 .. 178.8 |
| tt / 27 C / 3.30 V, main | 294.96 | 393.4 | 151.82 |

The tt/27 C/3.30 V point (5 ns step) reproduces the converged ~296 mA /
395 V/ms of `20261007-ss-351-peak-event` to the step-size error that record
tabulates (292.9 mA at 5 ns).

The peak moves with the corner (slow corners and low temperature release the
hold later and harder), but it moves identically in both DUTs. The EN-edge
(100-101 us) is never the peak: the earliest peak is at 135.3 us, so the
mechanism of the earlier record (hold release, then the current-limit clamp
trip) holds across the matrix by timing and by the DUT-to-DUT agreement. The
per-node waveform mechanism was shown only at tt/27 C/3.30 V in that record;
elsewhere it is **inferred from the timing, not re-traced** (unverified).

`dV/dt` is large (up to 2.1 kV/ms) in the 0.5 ohm ESR configurations because
the maximum includes the ESR voltage step at hold release (I x ESR), not only
the capacitor charging slope. This is a measurement property shared by both
DUTs.

### Time-step and window checks

- **Window**: the latest peak is at 284.9 us, the latest event peak at
  178.8 us, both inside the 400 us window; all measurement windows include the
  event. The window is not extended.
- **Step**: `corners/20261105-ss-351-peak-conv-2n/` reruns the worst-case
  corner (ss, -40 C, 3.63 V, all five `EXTRA_CAPS` configs, both DUTs) at
  2 ns versus 5 ns. Peak Icap changes <= 0.02 %. `dV/dt` changes <= 1.2 %
  (largest in the 0.5 ohm ESR configs). One point moves its peak time
  (4.7 uF / 0.5 ohm: 173.458 us to 171.927 us) in **both** DUTs identically
  (two near-equal local maxima exchange), with Icap unchanged to 0.02 %. So
  the 5 ns matrix is converged to ~1 % on `dV/dt` and <0.1 % on Icap at the
  tested extreme; other corners were not rerun at 2 ns (unverified for them).

### The two small-peak points (not hold-release events)

Two points have no large hold-release event: `ff / 125 C / 2.97 V` and
`ff / 125 C / 3.63 V` with the 1 uF / 100 mohm / **no-load (1e9)**
configuration (peaks 3.39 / 3.43 mA at 278.3 / 284.9 us and 8.08 / 8.12 mA at
227.2 / 226.9 us, new / baseline). These peaks are on the order of the ramp
current (1 uF x 2 V/ms = 2 mA), not the clamp-limited spike. The DUTs differ
there by 1.3 % in peak (3.388 vs 3.432 mA at the first point) and by 6.6 us in
peak time, because the peak sits on a flat, slowly varying top where a 1 %
difference moves the maximum a long way. I did not trace why this corner does
not produce the spike (hot fast silicon, no load), so that cause is
**unverified**. It is the smallest peak in the matrix, so it cannot bear on
the inrush clauses either way.

## What this means for DR-0006 / DR-0037

- DR-0037's open bullet for #351 can be closed on this evidence: the servo
  neither increases nor decreases the startup peak, at any tested PVT or
  `EXTRA_CAPS` point. The "lower peak" must not be cited. **Whether to update
  that bullet, and DR-0037's ratification, is for the DR-0037 ratification
  decision; this record does not edit the DR or ratify it.**
- The DR-0006 inrush / current-limit-clearance clauses: the peaks (66 to 457 mA)
  are 1 to 7x the 62 mA worst-corner current limit those clauses are written
  against, in the baseline as well. Only the two no-load ff/125 C points (3.4,
  8.1 mA) are below it. This is the pre-existing known gap that DR-0006
  records; nothing is relaxed here and the clauses keep depending on the
  baseline's own number.
- `icap_peak_ma`, `isup_peak_ma` and `dvout_max_vpms` from `tran 1u 9m`
  (`run.sh`, `klt_run.py`, and records such as
  `20261016-ss-320-pvt-main`) remain a lower bound with a netlist-dependent
  error and must not be compared between netlists. The `klt_peak_run.py`
  figures here (<= 5 ns) are the ones to cite.
