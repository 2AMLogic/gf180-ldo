## Why there is no `summary.csv`

`sim/soft-start/testbench/summarize.py` reads every named `.meas` scalar out
of every log and treats a MISSING one (not merely an out-of-range one) as a
bench error, hard-failing the whole rollup rather than silently omitting a
row. At 19 of 163 points here, `m_t_startup`'s own `.meas` statement —
`TRIG v(en) VAL=1.4 RISE=1 TARG v(vout) VAL=1.764 RISE=1` — throws
`trig(TARG): out of interval` because `VOUT` never crosses 1.764 V at all
inside the full 9 ms transient, not merely after 3 ms. `summarize.py` is
shared, general-purpose tooling and this record does not patch it to paper
over a genuinely-missing measurement; that would hide exactly the kind of
information `sim/README.md`'s append-only rule exists to preserve. Instead,
this record's numbers below are read directly out of the 163 raw `.log`
files in `sim/soft-start/corners/20260906-230703-d3cb117/` by a one-off
script (not committed — the raw logs themselves are the evidence), and the
**Result** section states exactly which points that affects. The 19
`m_t_startup`-failed points are counted as SETTLED-ACCURACY failures above
(the harder failure mode: never converges within the window at all), which
is consistent with how every other clause on the same point is adjudicated
directly against the raw `.log`.
