## What the change is aimed at, and why it is the acquisition and not the hand-over

The soft-start clamp is not a comparator that either engages or does not; it
is a **feedback loop that closes through the output node**. Its loop gain
therefore carries the output pole, `1/(2π·Rload·C_eff)`, which at the top of
DR-0001's window (4.7 µF into 36 Ω) is **0.94 kHz** — right on top of the
~3.6 kHz that `Rl2_ss` (6.2 MΩ) and the bare 7.2 pF `Cm_ss` put at the clamp's
gain node `CLG`. Two poles within a decade, with a bare Miller capacitor's
right-half-plane zero also nearby, is a badly damped loop.

Waveform probe at `ff/-40 °C/2.97 V`, 4.7 µF/500 mΩ, 50 mA on the superseded
design (raw scratch, not committed — the numbers below are reproduced by any
re-run of that netlist):

| t after enable | what happens |
|---|---|
| 0–145 µs | `CLG` slews 0 → 2.215 V; `PASS_GATE` still pinned at `VIN`, `VOUT` = 0 |
| 145–235 µs | `Mclamp_bg_ss` releases `BG`; `BG` and (following it, +0.70 V) `PASS_GATE` fall towards conduction |
| ~235 µs | pass device turns on into an output that is now **0.145 V behind** `1.5 × SSR` |
| 235–250 µs | recovery slews `VOUT` at **4.49 V/ms**, i.e. 4.7 µF × 4.49 V/ms = **20.7 mA** — against a 0.65 V/ms steady ramp and a ratified 5 mA bound |

The steady ramp itself never comes close to the bound: 4.7 µF × 0.836 V/ms
(the fastest corner) is 3.9 mA. The whole failure is the overshoot of a
poorly-damped loop acquiring through the pass device's dead zone.

`Rz_ss` is the textbook fix and the same `Rz`/`Cc` idiom `error_amp` already
carries.

## Where this change makes the bench worse: the 1 mΩ groups

`0.33 µF / 1 mΩ` is out of scope for issue #43 (its AC bullet 4) and is
`#51`'s territory. **Its pass counts do not change** — 16/20 on inrush,
overshoot and peak supply in both arms — but the four points that were already
failing get **worse**, and that is a real regression which this record does not
paper over:

| corner | inrush base → new | overshoot base → new | ripple base → new |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ss_-40c_3.63v_0.33u_0.001_36` | 198.32 → **314.86 mA** | 2.4445 → **2.6267 V** | 0.4798 → **1.0262 V** |
| `res_ss_-40c_3.63v_0.33u_0.001_36` | 167.11 → **311.89 mA** | 2.0662 → 2.3181 V | 0.4178 → 0.4095 V |
| `res_ss_-40c_2.97v_0.33u_0.001_36` | 155.84 → **205.38 mA** | 2.3182 → 2.4162 V | 0.7083 → 0.8438 V |
| `ss_-40c_2.97v_0.33u_0.001_36` | 113.68 → **157.11 mA** | 2.2258 → 2.3231 V | 0.6210 → 0.7288 V |

(Peak supply current at those same four points: 247.21 → 369.26, 217.16 →
368.38, 205.11 → 258.08 and 159.35 → 205.16 mA.)

The mechanism is understood and is the direct cost of the fix: `Cm_ss` sits
between `CLG` and `PASS_GATE`, so it is *also*, incidentally, extra `Cgd` on
the pass device — compensation the **main** loop has been getting for free
since #38. Putting `Rz_ss` in series removes that above ~14 kHz. At every
output network where the main loop is stable this is invisible (the settled
ripple stays at 1e-9 V and every in-scope pass count improves or holds); at the
1 mΩ ESR edge, where `sim/loop-stability` already reports the main loop as
failing, it makes an existing limit cycle larger.

The complementary 1 mΩ group moves the *other* way — `4.7 µF / 1 mΩ` inrush
improves 13.34…24.10 → 9.54…16.50 mA — so this is specifically the small-cap,
low-ESR cell, which is the same cell DR-0015 and DR-0016 both already name as
the design's hardest.

**No claim is made here about the 1 mΩ groups**, per issue #43's AC bullet 4.

## Selecting `Rz_ss`: a two-sided, measured window

`Rz_ss` is squeezed between two cliffs, both found by measurement, and the
usable band is narrow. Values are `ppolyf_u_3k` squares at W = 1 µm (~3.1 kΩ
each); all currents are peak capacitor current over the **full 9 ms** window.

The rows other than the as-built one are **rejected candidates measured as
scratch** — single points re-run from this same bench's deck against a
locally-patched netlist, not committed under `corners/`, since per
`sim/README.md` only the design that ships is characterised as evidence. Each
is reproducible by changing `r_length` on `XRz_ss` in
`design/netlist/ldo_core.spice` and re-running the named corner; the as-built
column is the only one that also appears in this record's committed logs.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (superseded design) | 20.75 mA | 6.78 mA | 7.63 mA | 1.48 mA |
| 250 sq / 0.78 MΩ | 16.98 | 4.85 | **5.34 FAIL** | — |

| 1200 sq / 3.72 MΩ | **178.0 FAIL** | — | — | — |

- The **lower** bound is the ratified inrush clause itself, at the 0 mA end:
  below ~400 squares that group goes back over 5 mA.
- The **upper** bound is hand-over stability at the *small*-capacitor end.
  This is the trap in tuning this network and is worth recording explicitly:
  the transient being tuned against happens 220–570 µs after enable, so a
  short screening window looks sufficient — and is not. Too much `Rz_ss`
  leaves the acquisition perfectly damped and destabilises the **hand-over**,
  milliseconds later. Screened over 1.2 ms, `Rz_ss` = 1200 squares looks like
  the best value in the sweep (10.9 mA); run to the full 9 ms it is 178 mA.
  **A 700-square build was run over the whole 163-point matrix** and turned a
  clean 1.48 mA hand-over at `ss_-40c_3.63v_0.33u_0.5_36` into a 279 mA
  excursion — an in-scope regression that only the full matrix exposes.
- 500 squares sits in the middle of the 400–600 band, ~30 % below the smallest
  value measured to fail, and is preferred over 700 even though 700 buys a
  further ~5 % on the (still-failing) 4.7 µF group.

Shrinking `Cm_ss` instead was also tried (60×60 → 15×15 with no `Rz_ss` takes
the worst 4.7 µF point 20.75 → 11.98 mA) and **rejected**: it makes the
0.33 µF/1 mΩ regression above substantially larger, and it does not clear the
4.7 µF group either way, so it buys a regression and no clause.

## Overall

**Overall (Startup, ratified row): PASS on ramp rate, overshoot, inrush and
peak-supply-current at 63/63 points of the nominal 1 µF / 100 mΩ / 50 mA main
matrix and at 20/20 points of both the 0.33 µF / 500 mΩ group and the 0 mA
group. FAIL on `t_startup <= 3 ms` at 48/63 main-matrix points (DR-0006,
unmoved and measured to be unmoved) and on inrush + peak-supply-current at the
4.7 µF end of DR-0001's capacitor window (0/20 and 2/20 respectively, both
pre-existing).**

Against issue #43's acceptance criteria specifically:

- **AC1** (0.33 µF and 4.7 µF at 500 mΩ pass inrush, overshoot and
  current-limit clearance at every corner): **NOT MET.** 0.33 µF/500 mΩ passes
  all three, 20/20 each. 4.7 µF/500 mΩ passes overshoot 20/20, improves inrush
  by 1.4× (20.75 → 14.86 mA worst) but stays 0/20, and is 2/20 on peak supply
  in both arms.
- **AC2** (the nominal 1 µF / 100 mΩ 63-point matrix passes the same three
  clauses at every corner): **MET.** 63/63 on all three, from 41/63 on inrush.
- **AC3** (no ratified clause relaxed): **MET.**
  `sim/soft-start/testbench/summarize.py` is byte-identical to the superseded
  record's; every bound in the tables above is the ratified number.
- **AC4** (1 mΩ groups out of scope, no claim made): **honoured**, and the
  measured regression at 0.33 µF/1 mΩ is reported above rather than omitted.
