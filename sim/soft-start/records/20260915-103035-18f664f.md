## Why there is a partial, not a full, `summary.csv`

`sim/soft-start/testbench/summarize.py` treats a missing measurement as a
bench error and hard-fails the whole rollup (by design — see the module's
own docstring). Rather than patch that convention to paper over the 8
non-converged points (the same choice `20260906-230703-d3cb117.md` made),
this record's `summary.csv` under
`sim/soft-start/corners/20260915-103035-18f664f/` contains the **155**
points that converged, generated by a one-off script reading the raw
`.log` files directly (not committed, per the same precedent); the 8
excluded points are named below and their raw logs are committed as
evidence in the same directory.

**Non-converged points (8)**: `ff_125c_3.30v_1u_0.1_36`,
`ff_125c_3.63v_0.33u_0.001_36`, `ff_125c_3.63v_0.33u_0.5_36`,
`ff_125c_3.63v_1u_0.1_1e9`, `ff_125c_3.63v_1u_0.1_36`,
`ff_125c_3.63v_4.7u_0.001_36`, `ff_125c_3.63v_4.7u_0.5_36`,
`sf_125c_3.63v_1u_0.1_36`. Every one is `ngspice`'s own
`trig(TARG): out of interval` on the `m_t_startup` `.meas` statement —
`v(vout)` never crosses 1.764 V at all inside the 9 ms window, not merely
after 1.8 ms. `m_vout_settled` still resolves at each of these points:
`ff_125c_3.63v_1u_0.1_36` reads 1.56863 V (13% low), matching
`20260906-230703-d3cb117.md`'s independent characterization of the same
corner (1.569 V) to five significant figures — direct confirmation this is
the pre-existing #191 settled-leakage finding, not a new failure mode this
record's resize introduces.

## Against the ratified row (DR-0006, for reference — not this record's claim)

The currently-ratified Startup row (≤ 1 V/ms, inrush/clearance at 4.7 µF,
±2% within 6 ms) is **not met** by this resize — it is not supposed to be:
DR-0024 proposes replacing it, not satisfying it. Stated for completeness:
ramp rate exceeds 1 V/ms at every one of the 155 converged points (the
resize's entire purpose); settling at 6 ms passes 155/155 (a strict subset
of the 1.8 ms result above, since every point that meets 1.8 ms also meets
6 ms). `README.md` is unchanged by this record; the ratified row remains the
authority on what is currently required until DR-0024 is ratified.

**Overall (Startup, ratified row, DR-0006): FAIL on ramp rate (155/155 points
now exceed the ratified ≤ 1 V/ms bound — this resize's deliberate purpose,
pending DR-0024's ratification) and unchanged/FAIL on inrush, clearance and
monotonicity (pre-existing #191 regression, not attributable to this
resize). PASS on the ratified ≤ 6 ms settling clause (155/155, superseded
in ambition but not violated by the 1.8 ms figure this record proposes).**

**Overall (Startup, DR-0024 proposed bounds, not yet ratified): PASS on
ramp rate (155/155 ≤ 5 V/ms) and settling (155/155 ≤ 1.8 ms) — the two
clauses this record's resize targets. FAIL on inrush, current-limit
clearance and monotonicity — the pre-existing, already-tracked issue #191
regression this record's A/B test (below) confirms is not attributable to
the resize.**

## Pre-existing #191 regression: A/B confirmation

Same-corner comparison, `sf_-40c_2.97v_1u_0.1_36`, ran on the identical
host/ngspice binary, differing **only** in `design/netlist/ldo_core.spice`'s
`Css`/`Ch_ss`/`Cr_ss` values:

| netlist | `m_icap_peak_ma` | `m_isup_peak_ma` |
|---|---|---|
| `origin/main` (pre-#212, unresized) | **231.076** | 234.498 |
| this record (resized) | **271.919** | 274.859 |

Both far over the 5 mA inrush budget and the 52 mA clearance ceiling, at the
same order of magnitude, on a device (`XMgma_ss`/`XMgmb_ss`/.../the
FB-injection transconductor chain) this record's resize does not touch — the
resize changes only `Css`/`Ch_ss`/`Cr_ss`, entirely outside that chain.
Nominal corner, same comparison, `tt_27c_3.30v_1u_0.1_36`:

| netlist | `m_icap_peak_ma` | `m_dvout_min` (monotonicity) |
|---|---|---|
| `origin/main` (pre-#212, unresized) | 1.195 | −5.1e-5 (clean) |
| this record (resized) | 4.760 | −6.3e-7 (clean) |

Both clean at nominal, both far over budget at the skewed corner — matching
`design/ldo_softstart.sch`'s own "KNOWN, UNRESOLVED REGRESSION (#191)" note
("clean at tt/27 C/3.30 V ... but NOT at most other points"). This is not
new information; it is this record's own reproduction of an already-tracked
defect, included so the inrush/clearance/monotonicity failure counts above
are not misread as this record's own regression.

## What this record does NOT claim

- It does not claim the inrush, current-limit-clearance, or monotonicity
  sub-clauses pass, at any bound. They do not, and the cause is the #191
  regression, not this record's resize.
- It does not claim DR-0024 is ratified, or that `README.md`'s Startup row
  has changed. DR-0024's `Status` is `proposed`; ratification is a human
  merge per the 2026-08-19 ratification-via-PR policy.
- It does not claim `sim/startup`'s separate C_eff = 4.7 µF-anchored
  testbench has been re-run against this resize. See DR-0024's Consequences
  section for why that is an explicit, named gap rather than a silent one.
- It does not claim `sim/quiescent-current` or `sim/loop-stability` were
  re-run. `Css`/`Ch_ss`/`Cr_ss` carry no DC current in any state (each is in
  series with a capacitor or terminates a ramp/delay node) and neither
  compensation network is touched, so by the same construction argument
  `design/ldo_softstart.sch`'s own note makes for `Iq`, this resize cannot
  move either measurement — but "cannot by construction" is not the same
  claim as "measured here," and no measurement of either is made in this
  record.
