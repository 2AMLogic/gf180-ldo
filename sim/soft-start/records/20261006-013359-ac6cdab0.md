## Result up front

- **T1-T5 are bit-for-bit where they were**, 63 corners, re-measured:
  T1 1/63, T2 0/63, T3 0/63, T4 1/63, **T5 63/63**. Residual injection at
  hand-over 35.27 ... 1432.95 nA, release `V(SSR)` 1.200 ... 2.025 V,
  settled `Vout` error -1.780 ... -0.500 mV, transconductance
  -4.530 ... -3.522 uA/V: every range equals `20260918-190207-8a59d23`.
  DR-0035 moved the settled ceiling, not the hand-over transfer. **No
  regression, and no improvement: T1-T4 absolute-accuracy targets remain
  unmet (DR-0023 R4 trigger, unchanged by this issue).**
- **All 163 transient points converge**; steady ramp rate max 3.4111 V/ms
  (was 3.4111), still under the proposed 5 V/ms. `t_startup` worst
  1.2872 ms over the 1 uF-and-up 36 ohm points (was 1.2872 ms).
- **The hold-release acquisition transient got worse, and this is a cost of
  DR-0035 disclosed here, not hidden.** At nominal 1 uF / 100 mOhm / 36 ohm
  (the ratified row's own condition) `icap_peak` worst moved
  314.9 -> 415.6 mA (`ss_-40c_3.63v`); over the whole 163 points worst
  `icap_peak` 481.0 mA (was ~385 mA, both at `res_ss_-40c_3.63v` at
  0.33 uF / 1 mOhm). The nominal-cap `vout_settled` and overshoot are
  unchanged (overshoot max 1.7995 V).
- **The 0.33 uF / 1 mOhm DR-0001 window corner is worse**: points with
  overshoot above +2% (1.836 V) went from 4 to 9 and points ringing above
  18 mV pp at the end of the enable window from 4 to 9
  (new: `ff_-40c_3.63v`, `tt_-40c_2.97v`, `tt_-40c_3.63v`,
  `res_ss_125c_2.97v`, `res_ss_125c_3.63v`; the four `ss` / `res_ss` -40C points
  were already in the old set; all at 0.33 uF / 1 mOhm; the two lists are
  identical). The worst `vout_max_en` there is 3.030 V (`ss_-40c_3.63v`), was
  2.844 V (`res_ss_-40c_3.63v`). Settled `Vout` outside +/-2% at that same corner: 2 points before
  (`ss_-40c_2.97v` 1.595 V, `res_ss_-40c_2.97v` 1.916 V) and 2 now
  (`ss_-40c_3.63v` 2.062 V, `res_ss_-40c_3.63v` 1.883 V): the failure moved
  from the low-supply to the high-supply edge of the same capacitor corner.
  This bench's "Startup" verdict was FAIL before (the 0918 record) and is
  unchanged in kind; this is a follow-up for the hold-release behavior at the
  extreme capacitor corner, not a closure.
- **The ratified-row bench `sim/startup` (81 points) is unaffected**: it
  records 0 failures on this DUT
  (`sim/startup/records/20261006-004902-ac6cdab.md`).
