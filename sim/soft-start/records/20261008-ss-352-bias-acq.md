## Results (tested conditions only)

Window-end grading uses the declared tolerances: VX within +/-1 % of VREF
over the last 40 us, bias within 1 % of VREF/R, startup branch current
<= 1 % of the servo's bias at window end.

| scenario | cells graded (63 corners x 2 servos x windows) | verdicts |
|---|---|---|
| `op` | 126 | 126 ACQUIRED |
| `op_nodeset` (hint toward inactive state) | 126 | 126 ACQUIRED |
| `encycle` (parts 1-4; 4 windows) | 504 | 504 ACQUIRED |
| `powerup` | 126 | 126 ACQUIRED |
| `forced` (pinned inactive start, then released) | 126 | 126 ACQUIRED |
| `vreflate` | 126 | 126 ACQUIRED |
| `negctl` (startup devices disabled) | 126 | 126 INACTIVE (as required) |

- **No persistent inactive state was found.** In `forced`, both servos are
  held with PB at VIN, VG and VX at VSS, and no `Rbias` current until 20 us.
  After release every corner and both servos reach the intended bias
  (`I(Mconv)` relative to VREF/R between 0.99978 and 0.99996). Acquisition
  time after release: `ldo_ilimit` 10.8-24.2 us, `ldo_softstart`
  25.4-56.8 us. The startup branch engaged (peak `I(Mst)` 1.3-3.8 uA) and
  turned off (`I(Mst)`/`I(Mconv)` within +/-0.9 % of zero at window end,
  well inside the 1 % tolerance, and below 0.3 % for `ldo_ilimit`).
- **The test can see a stuck loop.** `negctl` (same hold, `Mst`/`Mst_ss`
  gates tied to VSS) stays `INACTIVE` in all 126 cells: bias <= 0.13 % of
  VREF/R, VX about 1.2 V below VREF. So without the startup device the
  inactive state is a real, persistent state of this circuit, and the
  startup branch is what removes it. The `forced` result is therefore
  meaningful, not an artefact of the stimulus.
- **Power-up and late VREF.** `powerup` (VIN ramp 100 us, EN tied to VIN):
  acquisition 53-90 us. `vreflate` (VREF ramp starting at 20 us, 100 us
  long): acquisition 100-143 us after the window edge. All ACQUIRED. Two
  `vreflate` corners (`ss`, 125 C, 2.97 V) logged a `singular_matrix`
  event in the initial source-stepping DC solve and still graded
  ACQUIRED; this is a solver event, not an electrical failure.
- **EN cycling.** 100 us, 10 us and 1 us off-times and the initial
  enable all reacquire in every corner (acquisition <= 43.3 us; 0.13 us in
  some 1 us cases means VX never left the 1 % band, i.e. there was nothing
  to acquire). Intended EN = 0 shutdown: before the enable edge after a
  >= 100 us off-time, `I(Mconv)` is <= 1 % of the later bias (|I| <= 0.85
  nA against about 1.2 uA) in all 252 graded cells. The 1 us and 10 us cases
  are reported, not graded.
- **Solver diagnostic.** Initial DC solve path (ngspice log) for `op`: 2
  direct, 28 dynamic-gmin, 96 true-gmin (counted per servo row, so halve for
  corners: 1 / 14 / 48 of 63). The `nonconvergence` diagnostic is
  therefore **not specific to the reported tt/27 C/3.30 V point**; `klt
  sim` attached it to 94 of the 126 `op`/`op_nodeset` corner runs
  (188 of 252 rows in `pvt/op.csv`, two servo rows per corner). `op_nodeset` gives the same electrical
  root in every corner. In every case the final state is the intended
  bias, so the fallback is a solve-path property here, not an invalid or
  inactive result. The transient scenarios begin from the pinned or
  disabled state and are not affected by the diagnostic.

## What this does and does not support

Supports: under these stimuli (forced inactive start with PB near VIN and no
conveyor drive, normal power-up, late VREF, EN cycling at 3 time scales),
across 63 PVT corners including `res_ff`/`res_ss`, both servos always reached
the intended bias and the startup branch turned off. The startup branch is
necessary: without it the inactive state persists.

Does not support, and remains unverified before DR-0037 ratification:

- uniqueness of the operating point or absence of other states (finite
  sweep only);
- mismatch / Monte Carlo of the mirror and conveyor devices (not run);
- load, `Cout`/ESR and VIN-slew variants, and EN glitches shorter than 1 us;
- waveforms are retained for 8 reference corners (tt, ss x {27, -40} C x
  {3.30, 2.97} V), not all 63 (the other corners keep end-of-window
  measurements and logs);
- `VX - VREF` figures are quantized at the 10 uV level of the measurement
  output;
- the `op` DC measurement uses a dummy swept source; the fleet runner does
  not accept `measurements[].expr`;
- no corrective action is proposed: no defect was demonstrated, so no
  follow-up circuit issue was filed.
