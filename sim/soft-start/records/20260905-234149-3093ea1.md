## Comparison against the last recorded baseline: stale, not a clean #174 A/B

Unlike `current-limit` and `enable-shutdown` (whose same-day baselines this
PR also re-ran and found bit-identical), this bench's overshoot, inrush,
peak-supply-current and settled-ripple numbers **do not** match its own
last baseline — and they do not match in the direction of getting *worse*,
which is what a real regression would look like:

| Quantity | baseline, post-#51/#55 (`5ea33e8`, 2026-08-02) | this record | ratified |
|---|---|---|---|
| overshoot (`vout_max_en`) | 1.8409 … 1.9342 V (0/63 pass) | **1.80019 … 1.81204 V (63/63 pass)** | ≤ 1.836 V |
| inrush (`icap_peak_ma`) | 79.5 … 148.8 mA (0/63 pass) | **3.136 … 6.779 mA (41/63 pass)** | ≤ 5 mA |
| peak supply current | 128.7 … 199.5 mA (0/63 pass) | **50.461 … 50.917 mA (63/63 pass)** | ≤ 52 mA |
| settled ripple (`vout_pp_late`) | 0.088 … 0.262 V | **≤ 6.15e-9 V** | — |

This is **not** attributed to #174: the 2026-08-02 baseline explicitly
attributes its own bad numbers to a settled-state limit-cycle ring (measured
independently in `sim/enable-shutdown/records/20260802-023434-5ea33e8.md`)
that is unrelated to the soft-start clamp, and predates PR #78 / DR-0015
(2026-08-08), the `Mrza`/`Rza` adaptive-compensation shelf in
`design/error_amp.sch`. That same shelf is independently documented (this
repo's `sim/load-transient/records/`, "Update (#98, 2026-08-16)") to have
converted 41/45 FAILing corners on a *different* large-signal bench to
41/45 PASS by removing exactly this kind of settled-state ringing. The
pattern here — near-zero settled ripple and inrush/overshoot/peak-supply
all now comfortably inside their old failing ranges — is consistent with
the same fix, but this record does not perform the controlled pre/post
bisection `#98` did for `load-transient`, so that attribution is offered as
context, not as this record's claim. **A dedicated re-characterization of
this bench against the ratified Startup row, analogous to `#98`, is filed
as a follow-up** (see "Follow-up filed" below) rather than attempted here,
to keep this record's own claim — that #174 did not move anything —
checkable on its own terms.

## What #174 could plausibly have moved, and did not

- `t_startup > 3 ms` failure count: **48/63, identical to the last
  baseline** — this is the metric least likely to be perturbed by
  intervening compensation changes (it is dominated by the `R`×`C` time
  constant of the soft-start ramp generator, not by loop compensation), and
  it is exactly reproduced.
- Ramp rate and `t_startup` itself: both agree with the baseline to the
  precision either record reports.
- Safety-critical checks (all corners, all 163 points): `failed to reach
  1.764 V at all inside the enable window` — none; `soft-start ramp did not
  finish above VREF (clamp still engaged)` — none; `soft-start ramp
  capacitor not reset by disable` — none. Unchanged from the general shape
  reported by every prior soft-start record.
