## Attribution: DR-0015 is what moved these numbers, and the split is clean

### It moved the ring, and only the ring

The bisection separates the row's clauses into two disjoint groups, and the
split is exactly the one the physics predicts for a compensation change that
carries no DC current:

**Unmoved by DR-0015** — the ramp generator's own `R × C`:

- `slope_vpms`: maximum relative PRE→POST difference across all 63 main-matrix
  points is **7.1e-6**; 48 of 63 points print bit-identical values.
- `t_startup_ms`: maximum relative PRE→POST difference is **4.4e-6**; the
  `t_startup > 3 ms` failure count is **48/63 in both arms** — the same 48
  points, and the same count the 2026-08-02 baseline and every record before it
  reports. **DR-0006's dispute is untouched by DR-0015**, confirmed rather than
  assumed.
- Inrush at 4.7 µF / 1 mΩ, where the measured current is the *physical*
  `C_eff · dV_out/dt` of the ramp and not a ring: 13.33 … 24.09 mA PRE →
  13.34 … 24.10 mA POST, i.e. **+0.05 % … +0.17 % per point, 0/20 passing in
  both arms**. This is the control that shows the shelf cannot and does not
  reduce the real charge the regulator must push into a 4.7 µF load — which is
  why the ratified `inrush ≤ 5 mA at C_eff = 4.7 µF` clause still FAILs at
  every one of those 20 points on the shipped design.

**Moved by DR-0015** — the settled-state limit cycle:

- Corners still ringing at the end of the 8 ms enable window (`vout_pp_late`
  > 18 mV): **123 of 163 → 4 of 163**.
- Corners where the soft-start clamp gate has not fully released
  (`clg_end` more than 0.2 V below `V_IN`, i.e. `Mclamp_ss` still out of
  cutoff after hand-over): **54 of 163 → 0 of 163**.
- Main-matrix settled ripple: **50.0 … 208.7 mV → 0.35 … 6.15 nV** — a PRE/POST
  ratio of 9.7e6 … 2.8e8 per point, median 6.3e7.
- Main-matrix peak supply current collapses to the DC load current plus Iq
  (50.46 … 50.92 mA against a 50 mA load), i.e. there is no longer *any*
  settled-state current excursion to measure.

Because the ring is what the window maxima `vout_max_en`, `icap_peak_ma` and
`isup_peak_ma` were catching — the 2026-08-02 baseline says so in its own
prose, and `sim/enable-shutdown/records/20260802-023434-5ea33e8.md` measured
that attribution independently at the time — removing the ring is what moves
all three at once. That is what the PRE arm now demonstrates directly on
*this* bench rather than by inference from another one.
