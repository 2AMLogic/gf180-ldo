- **Claim**: README.md Target specification, 'Dropout @ 50 mA' row (< 300 mV target, < 200 mV stretch); binding corner ss/125 C, binding test point Vin=2.10V per DR-0004 note 4. MEASUREMENT DEFINITION CHANGED BY #138 / DR-0020 -- records before 20260816-103644-bea26f6 are NOT comparable to this one. Until #138 this deck solved a single .op at Vin=2.10V and reported vdrop_mv = (v(vin)-v(vout))*1e3. Because the ratified test point IS Vout_nom + the ratified bound (2.10 = 1.800 + 0.300), that expression is identically 300 mV + (1.800 V - Vout) for as long as the loop is in regulation: it reports the DC regulation error offset by exactly the bound, not the dropout voltage, and it can read below 300 mV only if Vout sits ABOVE its own setpoint -- which a negative-feedback loop with finite DC gain cannot do. That is why it FAILed 27/27 corners by 0.476-1.014 mV, and why no pass-device resize clears it (2x width moves the reading to 300.575 mV, 4x to 300.574 mV -- an asymptote above the bound; see DR-0020). The bound is NOT relaxed. vdrop_mv is now the dropout voltage proper: Vin is swept down through the regulation knee at the full 50 mA load and vdrop_mv is the Vin - Vout headroom at the point where Vout has fallen to the ratified -2% accuracy edge (1.764 V = 1.800 V - 2%, README note 2). In dropout Vout tracks Vin with a slowly varying offset, so the reading is insensitive to the exact threshold: a -1% (1.782 V) threshold moves tt/27C by ~2 mV. The ratified Vin=2.10V binding test point survives as its own gate -- vout_at_210_mv must sit inside the ratified +/-2% window at that headroom, which is the direct statement of 'dropout <= 300 mV at Vin = 2.10 V'. This remains a DC/loop-closure-only measurement: it does not account for stability (see sim/loop-stability), so it is never final sign-off on its own. #40's convergence note still applies: a cold-start Newton-Raphson solve of this closed, high-gain loop at full load has more than one KCL/KVL-satisfying fixed point -- with an all-zero initial guess several corners converge 'successfully' onto a non-physical point where the pass device is off and the 50 mA sink has no DC path except parasitic junction leakage, driving Vout tens of volts negative. The nodeset hint below biases the sweep's first operating point into the physical basin, and each subsequent sweep point starts from its predecessor's solution; the tanh-bounded sink in the deck deletes the sub-ground branch outright rather than hiding it. Because a swept knee measurement is only as trustworthy as the sweep is well-behaved, the 1.764 V crossing is located twice (RISE=1 and RISE=LAST since #472; FALL=1 and FALL=LAST before) and knee_uniqueness_mv gates their difference at zero: a single artifact point inserts a spurious crossing pair and would otherwise be interpolated as a false knee. Every corner of the shipped netlist crosses exactly once. Update (#174, 2026-09-05, spec/decision-records/DR-0021): ldo_core's VREF was promoted from an internal net -- driven by Vref1, an ideal 1.2 V vsource instantiated INSIDE the subcircuit -- to a seventh top-level port, and the identical ideal source moved into this deck. No device was added, removed or resized, so this is an electrical no-op by construction: an ideal vsource is an ideal vsource wherever it is declared, its return was already the cell's VSS port, and it is an AC short to ground in both placements. It is not asserted on that argument alone -- origin/main at ec9e530 was extracted to a scratch tree and this bench was run on BOTH trees back to back, on the same host with the same ngspice and the same pinned PDK, and the per-corner measurement lines compared field by field; DR-0021's 'Evidence' section tabulates the result. Records minted from this deck before 2026-09-05 carry the pre-#174 six-port DUT netlist sha and are superseded on that ground alone, not because any number in them was wrong; per sim/README.md's append-only rule they are left untouched. Update (#472): the Vin sweep now runs RISING (1.75 V -> 2.20 V) instead of falling. With the falling sweep the cold-start operating point at the top of the range (Vin = 2.20 V) and the next several points hopped between the physical branch and a pass-device-off branch (Vout ~ 0.14 V or ~ -0.08 V, loop ~ Vin) at ff/-40 C, ss/27 C, fs/27 C, sf/27 C and res_ff/27 C -- 6 of the first 10 points at ff/-40 C -- so the first falling crossing landed at 2.185-2.190 V, the knee guard read 259-323 mV and vdrop_mv 420-426 mV (records 20261021-013226-7c57744). Starting the sweep at 1.75 V puts the first operating point in dropout, where the pass device is fully on and the only DC solution is the physical one, and each later point continues from a physical neighbour. The measurement definition, the 1.764 V threshold, the 300 mV and +/-0.001 mV bounds and the Vin = 2.10 V gate are unchanged; the knee is simply located on the rising crossing. ## Reading

- #472 change: the Vin sweep now runs rising (`dc Vsup 1.75 2.20 0.005`, knee located with `RISE=1` / `RISE=LAST`). Bounds, the 1.764 V threshold and the Vin = 2.10 V gate are unchanged; the 300 mV and +/-0.001 mV bounds were not relaxed.
- Cause, measured on a single-corner local `ngspice -b` of `ff_-40c` (the cold-start deck of the earlier record, per-point `v(vout)`): with the falling sweep, 8 of the first 10 points (Vin = 2.200, 2.195, 2.190, 2.180, 2.175, 2.170, 2.165 and 2.155 V; rows 0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9) sit on a pass-device-off state (Vout = +0.141 V or -0.082 V, `loop` within 10 mV of Vin, 4.3 mA or ~0 delivered) and the others on the physical branch (Vout = 1.7995 V, 50 mA), and from Vin = 2.150 V down every point is physical. The first falling 1.764 V crossing is therefore between the physical point at 2.185 V and the bad point at 2.180 V (2.1849 V), not at the knee (1.862 V). The curator's hypothesis (only the first one to three points are affected) is refuted: the hopping extends over ten points. The `nodeset` (`loop=0.7`) is not near the physical `loop` value at 2.2 V (about 1.06 V at Vin = 2.185 V), so it does not hold the branch.
- Why a rising sweep is not another seed: it removes the cold start at the top of the range. At Vin = 1.75 V the part is in dropout with the pass device fully on, and each later point continues from a physical neighbour. No per-corner or per-point hint is added. The result is not path-lucky: all 27 corners report `knee_uniqueness_mv` = 0, and the 22 corners that were already valid agree with the earlier record to within rounding.
- Dropout is 97.5-193.2 mV against the 300 mV bound; the binding corner remains ss/125 C. The five repaired corners read `ff_-40c` 97.535, `fs_27c` 156.849, `ss_27c` 166.505, `sf_27c` 120.799 and `res_ff_27c` 137.156 mV, consistent with their 27 C and -40 C neighbours.
- Every corner still reports a `nonconvergence` diagnostic (a dynamic-gmin attempt that fails before the solve completes); values are produced at all 27.
- DC/loop-closure measurement only; not stability sign-off (see `sim/loop-stability`).
