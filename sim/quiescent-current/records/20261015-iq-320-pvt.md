## Result

80 of 81 points per DUT are physical (vout_full ~ 1.8 V, 50 mA delivered) and
pass. One point, `ss_-40c_2.97v`, is **not** a valid measurement for either
DUT and is excluded from the table below.

| quantity (80 valid points) | new, max (corner) | baseline, max | new - baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iq enabled, no load (uA) | 28.303 (ff, 125 C, 3.63 V) | 27.388 | +0.42 .. +0.92 |
| Iq enabled, 50 mA load (uA) | 29.631 (ff, 125 C, 3.63 V) | 28.715 | +0.42 .. +0.92 |
| Iq disabled (uA) | 0.277 (ff, 125 C, 3.63 V) | 0.276 | +0.0001 .. +0.0013 |

**Delta: the servo costs +0.42 to +0.92 uA of enabled Iq** over the PVT matrix
(+0.60 uA at tt/27 C/3.30 V, matching the earlier probe). Disabled current
stays below 0.3 uA at every valid point, so EN = 0 gating holds at nA level.

## Reading

- **Headroom is thin.** The binding corner (ff, 125 C, 3.63 V) is 29.631 uA
  at full load against the 30 uA bound, 1.2 % under it; the baseline was
  28.715 uA, so the bound is met with or without the servo, but the servo
  uses roughly a third of the remaining margin. Not a pass with room to spare.
- **`ss_-40c_2.97v` is a false convergence, in both DUTs.** It reports
  vout_full = -0.055 V (new) / -0.038 V (baseline) with ~0.002 / 0.004 mA
  delivered, i.e. the sub-ground state described in the testbench's claim
  (#304/#325), and negative Iq. It is graded `False` for both DUTs. This
  record therefore does **not** establish Iq at the ss/-40 C/2.97 V corner;
  it is a harness limitation shared by baseline and new, not a servo
  regression.
- **`nonconvergence` diagnostic**: raised on 65 of 81 new points and 9 of 81
  baseline points. Values were produced and, apart from the point above, are
  physical. As in `20261015-ilim-320-pvt` this is not resolved here (#352).
- Disabled-state current is an operating-point measurement; it does not
  speak to loop stability or soft-start behavior with the new DUT.
