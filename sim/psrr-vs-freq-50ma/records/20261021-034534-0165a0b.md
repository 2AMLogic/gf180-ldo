- **Claim**: README.md Target specification, 'PSRR' row (DR-0004 amendment A7): >50dB @ 1kHz and >20dB @ 100kHz, C_eff=1uF nominal -- evaluated here at the 50 mA FULL-LOAD condition. This is the load condition sim/psrr-vs-freq/ disclosed as an open gap: that deck is scoped to the 1 mA light-load point (the binding one per README.md note 1) and its tb_psrr.spice header records why the two load points cannot share one deck -- ngspice's per-.meas result vectors go out of scope as soon as a later analysis becomes the current plot, so an 'alter'-and-re-.ac deck silently re-reports the first .ac's numbers. This slug closes that gap the way sim/dropout-vs-load/ already handles its own fixed full-load point: a sibling experiment with the same DUT, the same stimulus and output network, the same full PVT sweep, and its own record carrying its own separate verdict. The two verdicts are deliberately NOT merged -- read this record for the 50 mA condition and sim/psrr-vs-freq/'s record for the 1 mA condition. The load sink is the compliance-limited sink #46 introduced (Vilc/Bload/Vlmeas): it draws its commanded current for VOUT well above ground and nothing at or below it, which deletes the sink-sustained unphysical sub-ground DC solution #40 found rather than biasing the solver away from it. That solution is the dominant risk at this load -- a larger demanded current biases the unphysical root harder -- so dc_vout_v and dc_iload_ma below read the .op plot and are gated at every corner: a PSRR number is only admissible as evidence if the operating point it was linearized about is the physical one and the sink really delivered 50 mA. No ratified threshold was changed by this experiment. Update (#174, 2026-09-05, spec/decision-records/DR-0021): ldo_core's VREF was promoted from an internal net -- driven by Vref1, an ideal 1.2 V vsource instantiated INSIDE the subcircuit -- to a seventh top-level port, and the identical ideal source moved into this deck. No device was added, removed or resized, so this is an electrical no-op by construction: an ideal vsource is an ideal vsource wherever it is declared, its return was already the cell's VSS port, and it is an AC short to ground in both placements. It is not asserted on that argument alone -- origin/main at ec9e530 was extracted to a scratch tree and this bench was run on BOTH trees back to back, on the same host with the same ngspice and the same pinned PDK, and the per-corner measurement lines compared field by field; DR-0021's 'Evidence' section tabulates the result. Records minted from this deck before 2026-09-05 carry the pre-#174 six-port DUT netlist sha and are superseded on that ground alone, not because any number in them was wrong; per sim/README.md's append-only rule they are left untouched. Update (#325, 2026-09-24, carrying #304's measurement across from sim/psrr-vs-freq): the 'deletes' sentence above is scoped deliberately -- the compliance bound deletes the SINK-SUSTAINED sub-ground root and nothing else. Below the knee the sink is exactly 0 A and 0 S (f(VOUT) underflows to 0.0 in IEEE double for every VOUT <= -0.7530773732699249 V), so the load network is absent from the DC equations there: a sub-ground dc_vout_v reported together with dc_iload_ma = 0 is outside the bound's reach by construction, and no knee or softness value can delete it because the sink has no term left in the equations to remove. The sibling 1 mA deck reported exactly that at fs_125c_3.63v in record 20260923-084323-a6c95f8 (dc_vout_v = -64.75 V, dc_iload_ma = 0) on the DUT netlist of the day, and #304 measured the mechanism: not a second physical solution but a false convergence on the continuation ladder's first rung (dynamic gmin stepping), accepted by SPICE's RELATIVE convergence test 64 V from ground. That is why dc_vout_v and dc_iload_ma are gated as a PAIR rather than either one alone -- a sub-ground reading alongside the COMMANDED current is the sink-sustained root the bound deletes, alongside ZERO delivered current it is a state the bound was never in a position to delete. The deck is deliberately unchanged on that account: #304 measured that knee tuning is coin-flipping (a strictly LOOSER bound 'fixes' the same corner, so the knee is not the mechanism) and that a load-side sub-ground clamp is worse still (it adds a genuine root at VOUT = -0.4994 V that a cold start reaches). See sim/harness/README.md, 'What the bound cannot reach', for the evidence and for the reltol-ladder recipe that tells a false convergence from a genuine second root. Update (#478): the sink is now also gated on the supply, g(VIN) = 0.5*(1+tanh((VIN-2.0)/0.05)), so ngspice's source stepping reaches the 50 mA point by continuation instead of landing on the pass-device-off state (Vout 0.145 V, 5.0 mA delivered) that ss / -40 C / 2.97 V converged to in both the op and the ac request (record 20261021-023703-7d3b2a0). A preceding .dc sweep of Vilc, the #472 fix for the sibling benches, was measured and does not reach the .ac state: .ac re-solves its own operating point from a cold start and ignores the sweep. Nothing is seeded per corner; the measurements and every bound are unchanged. Measurement definitions and bounds are `sim/psrr-vs-freq-50ma/testbench/tb.json`'s, unchanged.

## Reading

Reading (written by hand, #478).

- **What changed against `20261021-023703-7d3b2a0`**: the DUT (`4f99a1a9…`), backend (`klt sim` batch fleet) and open_pdks revision (`f6eeac7`, staged) are the same as that record's; the only difference is the bench, `tb_psrr_50ma.spice`, whose load sink gained a second smooth gate on the supply (see its header and tb.json's claim, Update #478). The generated paragraph above names backend and PDK as differing; for this pair they do not.
- **Reproduction (single-corner local run, `klt sim --backend local`, `ss/-40C/2.97V`, before the change)**: both the `.op` and the `.ac` request report `nonconvergence`. Dynamic and true gmin stepping and source stepping all fail, ngspice falls through to `Transient op`, and the reported point is the pass-device-off state (Vout 0.145 V, 5.00 mA delivered; PSRR 143.3 dB at 1 kHz, 116.7 dB at 100 kHz). The step into 50 mA is the full 0 -> 50 mA at supply levels that source stepping scales together.
- **What does not work**: a `dc Vilc 1m 50m 7m` sweep ahead of the `.ac` (the #472 fix for the load/line/dropout benches), run as a `klt sim` `analysis_steps` sequence (`dc` then `ac`). The sweep itself converges (true gmin stepping at 1 mA), but the `.ac` step re-solves its operating point from a cold start, fails the same four rungs, and reports 143.30 dB again. So a sweep cannot be the continuation for an `.ac` request.
- **The change**: with the sink gated on VIN (`0.5*(1+tanh((VIN-2.0)/0.05))`, 1 - g < 1e-16 and dg/dVIN * 50 mA < 1e-15 S at 2.97 V), source stepping completes in both requests at this corner and the state is the physical one: Vout 1.79948 V, 50.000 mA, PSRR 80.73 dB at 1 kHz and 41.57 dB at 100 kHz, in line with `ss_-40c_3.30v` (77.2 / 42.5 dB) and `ss_-40c_3.63v` (70.1 / 43.4 dB). Nothing is seeded (no `.nodeset`/`.ic`, #308/#311) and no bound in tb.json moved.
- **The other 44 corners**: agree with `20261021-023703-7d3b2a0` to within rounding on every measurement except `psrr_100k_db` at `ff_125c_2.97v` (44.7587 vs 44.7578 dB, 0.9 mdB; it passes either way). The three remaining FAILs are the same real measurements as before and out of this issue's scope: `ff_125c_3.30v` and `ff_125c_3.63v` and `sf_125c_3.63v` miss `psrr_1k_db` (DR-0031, #302). The corner that was graded FAIL on a non-physical operating point now passes, so the bench is 42/45 against 41/45, all three remaining failures being physical.
- **Not established here**: that the gate is the only way to reach the physical point; it is the one that was measured. The 1 mA sibling `psrr-vs-freq` is unchanged and valid at all 45 corners without it.
