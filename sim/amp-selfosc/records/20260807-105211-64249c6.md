# Record 20260807-105211-64249c6

- **Record ID**: 20260807-105211-64249c6
- **Claim**: The precondition under which sim/loop-stability/'s Bode phase and gain margins are a stability test at all: DR-0001's 'PM >= 45 deg and GM >= 10 dB' criterion is only valid when the loop gain T(s) has no right-half-plane poles, i.e. when the forward path is stable with the LDO loop broken. design/error_amp.sch keeps a LOCAL feedback loop (Rz/Cc from N1 around M2P and the class-AB gate buffer back to OUT) closed in exactly that condition, so the precondition is a design property that has to be measured, not assumed. This bench measures it in the time domain, where no phase-unwrapping convention can hide it: with EN held high and the loop settled, the peak-to-peak of the amplifier's internal BG node and of the pass gate over a late, quiet window must be at the noise floor. This bench makes NO claim about the LDO loop's own margins -- that is sim/loop-stability/'s (issue #10's) claim, and it is the claim this one is a precondition for.

  - Stimulus: a 1 nA, 1 us current pulse into each pass gate at t = 10 us -- 190 us (~95 periods of the activity this bench catches) before the measurement window opens. See tb_amp_selfosc.spice. Nothing drives either instance inside the window.

The 200 ns ceiling is ~10 points per period of the ~500 kHz activity this bench exists to catch and is load-bearing rather than a performance knob: Gear/BDF integration is numerically damping, so a coarser ceiling damps the very signal being measured. Verified by hand at tt/27C/3.30V -- a 400 ns ceiling reports 37 nV pk-pk on the heavy branch where this setting reports 3.31 V. The coarse setting is the false negative; do not loosen it to make the matrix cheaper.
