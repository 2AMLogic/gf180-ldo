# Record 20260802-024900-950ffe9

- **Record ID**: 20260802-024900-950ffe9
- **Claim**: The precondition under which sim/loop-stability/'s Bode phase and gain margins are a stability test at all: DR-0001's 'PM >= 45 deg and GM >= 10 dB' criterion is only valid when the loop gain T(s) has no right-half-plane poles, i.e. when the forward path is stable with the LDO loop broken. design/error_amp.sch keeps a LOCAL feedback loop (Rz/Cc from N1 around M2P and the class-AB gate buffer back to OUT) closed in exactly that condition, so the precondition is a design property that has to be measured, not assumed. This bench measures it in the time domain, where no phase-unwrapping convention can hide it: with EN held high and the loop settled, the peak-to-peak of the amplifier's internal BG node and of the pass gate over a late, quiet window must be at the noise floor. This bench makes NO claim about the LDO loop's own margins -- that is sim/loop-stability/'s (issue #10's) claim, and it is the claim this one is a precondition for.

- **Corner matrix run**:
  - Process: tt, ff, ss, fs, sf
  - Temperature: -40 °C, 27 °C, 125 °C
  - Supply: 3.63 V
  - 15 point full-factorial grid (process × temperature × supply), 15 completed
  - **Subset of the mandated PVT matrix.** Gaps: supply: missing 2.97 V, 3.30 V.
  - Justification: Supply axis held at 3.63 V, the top of DR-0002's 2.97-3.63 V window. This bench is the most expensive point in sim/ (two ldo_core instances through 0.3 ms of Gear integration at a 200 ns ceiling, ~3 CPU-minutes per point), and the supply axis is already covered at FULL resolution by the frequency-domain detector this same finding adds: sim/amp-openloop/records/20260802-013956-bb0a991.md reports peak_excess_db above its bar at 81 of 81 points, i.e. at every temperature AND every supply. This bench's job is to confirm in the time domain, at every MOS corner and every temperature, what that AC row detects everywhere. 3.63 V is the supply at which the resonance measures largest (peak_excess_lightload_db is maximal at 3.63 V within every corner/temperature of the AC grid) and the supply at which sim/loop-stability/'s 15 residual gain-margin failures sit. The full 45-point grid is the correct follow-up once the compensation is re-solved and the expected answer is a PASS, which costs far less CPU because a settling loop does not force the timestep down.
- **Operating conditions**:
  - Load current: ~0.1 mA (Rload_light = 18 kOhm) and ~50 mA (Rload_heavy = 36 Ohm), both resistive -- see tb_amp_selfosc.spice
  - Output cap: 0.33 uF with 1 mOhm ESR on both branches: the a-priori worst point of DR-0001's window (smallest C_eff, no ESR zero)
  - Enable state: EN held at VIN for the whole run -- the transient starts from the settled regulating operating point, so the measured window contains no enable transient
  - Stimulus: a 1 nA, 1 us current pulse into each pass gate at t = 10 us -- 190 us (~95 periods of the activity this bench catches) before the measurement window opens. It is a defined SEED, not a drive: without it an unstable loop grows out of the integrator's truncation noise and how far it has grown by a given time is not reproducible (measured: 1.21 V pk-pk vs sub-microvolt on the same netlist in two windows). See tb_amp_selfosc.spice. Nothing drives either instance inside the window.
  - Timestep and window: tran 200n 0.3m 0.15m 200n, measured over 0.2-0.3 ms. The 200 ns ceiling is ~10 points per period of the ~500 kHz activity this bench exists to catch and is load-bearing rather than a performance knob: Gear/BDF integration is numerically damping, so a coarser ceiling damps the very signal being measured. Verified by hand at tt/27C/3.30V -- a 400 ns ceiling reports 37 nV pk-pk on the heavy branch where this setting reports 3.31 V. The coarse setting is the false negative; do not loosen it to make the matrix cheaper.
