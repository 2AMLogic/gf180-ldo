**Corners changed classification (in ↔ out of the 65–80 mA window, or in ↔
out of the ≤ 290 mW thermal bound) relative to the baseline: zero.** A
point-by-point diff of this run's `summary.csv` against `8e105a0`'s shows
every measured column bit-identical to within floating-point noise: of 63
rows, 2 differ, and only in `flat_0_vs_1500_pct` (a secondary,
non-pass/fail-gated column) at the 6th significant digit (e.g. `0.878608`
vs `0.878597` at `res_ff_27c_3.30v`) — consistent with DR-0021's documented
residual pattern (deleting one voltage source changes the MNA matrix's
dimension and pivot order, perturbing round-off in an op-point solve by an
amount far below any ratified bound). No `ilim_*`, `pshort_mw`,
`sense_margin_pct` or `vout_*` value moved by even that much.

- **Overall (current limit, ratified 65–80 mA window): FAIL** — 36/63
  corners outside the window (measured 62.0–93.8 mA), unchanged from the
  baseline. Assessed against the ratified window only; `DR-0005`'s proposed
  relaxed window is unratified (see References) and is not used here.
- **Overall (thermal, ratified ≤ 290 mW into a short): FAIL** — worst-case
  345.2 mW at `ff_125c_3.63v`, 12/63 corners exceed 290 mW, all at
  `Vin` ≥ 3.30 V in the `ff` / `res_ff` fast-nmos-resistor corner family —
  unchanged from the baseline.
- **Overall (never engages ≤ 50 mA / brickwall flatness / ±2 % window at
  50 mA): PASS** — unchanged from the baseline.

This is expected and confirms DR-0021's evidence argument: the
current-limit / thermal testbench is a DC / quasi-static V-I sweep, and
promoting `VREF` from an in-cell ideal source to a deck-level ideal source

arbitration node (`design/ldo_ilimit.sch`'s `CLG` / `Mclamp_bg`) settles to.
