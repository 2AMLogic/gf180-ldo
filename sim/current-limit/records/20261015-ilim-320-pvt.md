## Reading

- Every point is inside the DR-0005 window and inside the thermal bound, for
  both DUTs. The servo does not move the plateau: it tracks the idealized
  CCCS to a few microamps at all corners.
- **Margins are thin and unchanged by this change.** The window floor
  (`ss`, -40 C, 2.97 V: 62.025 vs 62.0 mA) and the thermal bound (345.598 vs
  346 mW) are both within 0.1 % of their limits, in the baseline too. This
  record shows the servo is no worse; it does not show the margins are
  comfortable.
- **The `nonconvergence` diagnostic is not resolved.** `klt sim` raised it on
  55 of 63 new points (1 of 63 baseline). Values were produced and graded,
  but the bias-acquisition question is tracked in #352 and its record
  (`sim/soft-start/records/20261008-ss-352-bias-acq.md`) does not close it.
- Not covered here: EN = 0 exact-zero current at the extremes, loop stability
  with the new DUT, soft-start ramp rate and `Iq` across PVT. `DR-0037`
  stays `proposed`.
