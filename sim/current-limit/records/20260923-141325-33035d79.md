- **Record ID**: 20260923-141325-33035d79

  `DR-0034` widened `Mpass` from 2 mm to 2.8 mm and `Msense` from `W=50u` to
  `W=70u` in the same commit, holding the 1/40 replica ratio exact. The prior

- **Claim**: `README.md` target-spec **"Current limit"** row as ratified by

## Where the width actually binds: the thermal row, at ≈ 3.8 mm

Measured on this deck at the binding `ff`/125 °C/3.63 V corner
(`NO_RECORD=1 CORNERS=ff TEMPS=125 SUPPLIES=3.63`), with the 1/40 `Msense`
replica ratio and equal per-finger width held exact at every point:

| `Mpass` width | dead-short onset | dissipation at `Vin_max` | vs. ratified ≤ 346 mW |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.00 mm (was shipped) | 95.1005 mA | 345.215 mW | passes, 0.785 mW margin |
| 2.72 mm | 95.1933 mA | 345.551 mW | passes, 0.449 mW |
| **2.80 mm (shipped, this record)** | **95.2028 mA** | **345.586 mW** | **passes, 0.414 mW** |
| 3.00 mm | 95.2259 mA | 345.670 mW | passes, 0.330 mW |
| 3.20 mm | 95.2482 mA | 345.751 mW | passes, 0.249 mW |
| 4.00 mm (`sim/devchar/CONCLUSIONS.md` §1's recommendation) | 95.3301 mA | 346.048 mW | **fails by 0.048 mW** |

The 2 mm, 3 mm and 4 mm rows reproduce `DR-0032`'s table to the printed digit
(it recorded 345.215 / 345.671 / 346.049 mW; the 0.001 mW differences are
rounding of the same solve). Linear in width over this range at
≈ 0.104 mW/mm, the ≤ 346 mW bound is reached at ≈ 3.8 mm — which is why
`DR-0034` states the thermal row, not gain margin, as the constraint that
would bind a *further* widening above the 3.0–3.2 mm gain-margin ceiling.
Every row but 2.80 mm is a candidate netlist that is **not** shipped: they are
`--no-write` single-corner runs, deliberately not minted as records, exactly
as `DR-0032` did (a head record for a netlist this repo does not ship would
hijack `sim/CHARACTERIZATION.md`'s most-recent-record-wins row selection).

Reproduction, from the repo root, for any width `W` (`Msense` = `W`/40):

```
# set Mpass to L=0.28u W=<W>u nf=40 in design/ldo_core.sch and Msense to
# L=0.28u W=<W/40>u nf=1 in design/ldo_ilimit.sch, then
python3 design/netlist.py
NO_RECORD=1 CORNERS=ff TEMPS=125 SUPPLIES=3.63 ./sim/current-limit/testbench/run.sh
```
