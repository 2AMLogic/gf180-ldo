### What this measurement contributed to #139's width decision

The 0.785 mW of Thermal-row margin above is one of the two bounds that decided
the pass-device width. The clamp's inverse-fold grows with `Mpass`'s width, so
the dead-short onset — and with it the dissipation — rises as the device is
widened, measured at the binding `ff_125c_3.63v` corner with the 1/40 `Msense`
ratio held exact throughout:

| `Mpass` width | dead-short onset | dissipation at `Vin_max` | vs. ratified ≤ 346 mW |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2 mm (shipped, this record)** | **95.1005 mA** | **345.215 mW** | **passes, 0.785 mW margin** |
| 3 mm | 95.2262 mA | 345.671 mW | passes, 0.329 mW margin |
| 4 mm (`sim/devchar/CONCLUSIONS.md` §1's recommendation) | 95.3302 mA | 346.049 mW | **fails by 0.049 mW** |

The 3 mm and 4 mm rows are single-corner `NO_RECORD=1 CORNERS=ff TEMPS=125
SUPPLIES=3.63` runs of this same deck against candidate netlists that were not
adopted; `DR-0032` records them, the reproduction commands, and the *other*
bound (DR-0018's ratified gain margin, which binds first at ≈ 2.5 mm).
