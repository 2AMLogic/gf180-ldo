# Record 20260801-012857-164ab42

- **Record ID**: 20260801-012857-164ab42
- **Claim**: `README.md` target-spec **"Current limit"** row — *65–80 mA over
  PVT, constant-current (brickwall) clamp; never engages for `I_load` ≤ 50 mA
  at any corner (binds ff / −40 °C, strongest pass drive); survives a
  continuous Vout = 0 short at Vin_max (note 5)*. Three separable sub-claims,
  each given its own verdict below, plus the **Thermal** row's
  *≤ 290 mW into a Vout = 0 short at the 80 mA limit ceiling*, which this
  bench measures directly because it is a consequence of the limit value.
  Issue #11.
- **Netlist provenance**: schematic — `design/netlist/ldo_core.spice`,
  exported by `design/netlist.py` from `design/ldo_core.sch` +
  `design/ldo_ilimit.sch` + `design/error_amp.sch`. Real amplifier (#9), real
  limit block (#11); the only idealizations in the loop are `Vref1` (an ideal
  1.2 V source standing in for a bandgap block that does not exist) and
  `Fbias` inside `ldo_ilimit` (a CCCS copying the real current through the
  real `Rbias` branch, standing in for the bandgap-referenced bias generator
  — see that schematic's "WHAT IS IDEALIZED HERE, AND WHY").
- **Corner matrix run**:
  - Process: tt, ff, ss, fs, sf, res_ff, res_ss
  - Temperature: −40 °C, 27 °C, 125 °C
  - Supply: 2.97 V, 3.30 V, 3.63 V (3.3 V ±10 %)
  - 63 points, full factorial, all completed.
  - **Subset reason**: `bjt_ff` / `bjt_ss` are omitted — the design contains
    no bipolar device, and the two sections change nothing in this netlist.
    Every process section that *does* appear in the netlist is swept,
    including the two resistor-only corners, which is the point: the
    resistor sections are what move this measurement.
- **Operating conditions**:
  - Load: two styles at every PVT point, both resistive or voltage-forced,
    never an ideal current sink (see the testbench header for why an ideal
    sink makes this circuit's operating-point solve ill-conditioned).
    *Regulation mode* — `Rload` = ∞ / 72 Ω / 36 Ω, i.e. 0 / 25 / 50 mA, with
    the forcing path absent. *V-I mode* — `Vout` forced to 1.764, 1.5, 0.9,
    0.3 and 0.0 V, tracing the whole limit characteristic down to a dead
    short, plus a 0→1.9 V sweep left in the raw log.
  - Output cap: none on this bench. The claim is DC: a capacitor is inert at
    DC and would only add a solve-time constant. The cap-conditioned claims
    (stability, transient) are #10's and #12's; the *transient* interaction
    between the limit and a charging output cap is measured in
    `sim/enable-shutdown/records/20260801-013308-164ab42.md`.
  - Enable state: enabled (`EN` = `VIN`) throughout.
  - Load current is **measured** (`Vlmeas` / `Vforce`), never assumed from
    the resistor value.
- **Statistical convention**: N/A — corner-matrix claim. **Device mismatch is
  not in this number.** The comparator pair's random offset directly shifts
  the threshold and this PDK's Monte Carlo is not exercised here; the
  measured spread below is therefore a *lower bound* on the as-built spread,
  which only strengthens the finding (see DR-0005).
- **Result**:

  **Sub-claim 1 — the limit never engages inside the rated 0–50 mA load:
  PASS at all 63 corners.** Worst-case onset is **62.01 mA** at
  ss/−40 °C/2.97 V, i.e. **+24.0 % above the 50 mA rated load**. Independent
  confirmation from the regulation-mode legs: `Vout` at a 50 mA resistive
  load is 1.7991–1.7996 V at every corner (inside ±2 % = 1.764–1.836 V), and
  the sense-domain margin — how far below the threshold voltage the sensed
  voltage still sits at 50 mA — is never smaller than **18.8 %**.

  **Sub-claim 2 — constant-current (brickwall), not foldback: PASS at all 63
  corners.** Between `Vout` = 1.5 V and a dead short the current changes by
  **+0.32 % to +2.30 %** — always *upward*, i.e. very slightly
  inverse-folded, never folded back. Nothing in this block reduces the limit
  as the output collapses, which is what note 5 requires and what makes
  startup into an already-loaded output possible (verified as an actual
  startup in the enable/shutdown record).

  **Sub-claim 3 — survives a continuous Vout = 0 short at Vin_max:
  PASS electrically, FAIL against the Thermal row's power number.** The
  forced-short operating point converges and the loop regulates at every
  corner (that is what sub-claim 2's numbers are). The dissipation it implies
  is **345.1 mW** at ff/125 °C/3.63 V, against the ratified
  *≤ 290 mW into a short* — see the FAIL below. Survivability itself is
  delegated to the package/integration spec by the Thermal row.

  **Sub-claim 4 — 65–80 mA over PVT: FAIL. 36 of 63 corners are outside the
  window** (all of ff / res_ff high, all of ss / res_ss low). Measured
  **62.01…93.80 mA = 77.90 mA ±20.4 %** against a window that is ±10.3 %.

  The measurement attributes that failure precisely, and **not to this
  design**:

  | corner group | resistor sections | I_lim range (mA) |
  |---|---|---|
  | tt | typical | 73.91 … 75.61 |
  | fs, sf (FET skew only) | typical | 73.85 … 75.66 |
  | ff | `res_ff` | 91.45 … 93.80 |
  | res_ff (resistors only) | `res_ff` | 91.33 … 93.69 |
  | ss | `res_ss` | 62.01 … 63.36 |
  | res_ss (resistors only) | `res_ss` | 62.08 … 63.39 |

  The FET-skew corners that hold resistors typical land on top of tt: the
  whole circuit's contribution — sense-ratio error, comparator behaviour,
  −40…125 °C, ±10 % supply — is **under ±1.2 %**. Change only the resistor
  section and the limit moves ±20 %, and `res_ff`/`res_ss` alone reproduce
  the ff/ss extremes to within 0.2 mA. The threshold is
  `41 · VREF · (Rref/Rbias) / Rsns`; the `Rref/Rbias` ratio cancels exactly,
  leaving one un-cancelled absolute on-chip resistance, and gf180mcu's
  tightest poly flavour still moves 38.7–40 % ff-to-ss
  (`sim/devchar/CONCLUSIONS.md` §2). Any untrimmed current limit is a voltage
  over a resistance, so **±10 % is not reachable on this PDK without trim** —
  the DR-0004 revisit trigger for this row, fired on measurement. See
  `spec/decision-records/DR-0005-current-limit-window.md`.

  **Threshold as a single number, with its centering rationale**: **74.9 mA**
  at tt/27 °C/3.30 V. Given a spread that is fixed at ±20 % by the PDK, the
  centering was chosen as the *lowest* value that still satisfies both
  ratified lower bounds at the worst corner — "never engages for
  `I_load` ≤ 50 mA" and the Startup row's "startup at full rated load stays
  ≥ 10 mA below the current limit", i.e. `I_lim` ≥ 60 mA everywhere — which
  simultaneously minimizes the short-circuit-power overage above. Centering
  low enough to clear 290 mW would put the worst-corner onset at ≈ 53 mA,
  6 % above the rated load and inside the sense path's own unmodelled
  mismatch, so the limit would nuisance-trip inside the rated range.

  **Overall: PASS on the safety-critical and behavioural halves of the row
  (never trips inside the rated load; brickwall; short-circuit regulation),
  FAIL on the ±10 % window and on the Thermal row's 290 mW consequence.**
  Not relaxed here: the failing rows are reported as failing and routed to a
  decision record.
