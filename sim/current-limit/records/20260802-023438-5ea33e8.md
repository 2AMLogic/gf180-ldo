## Result: every sub-claim keeps its previous verdict, and every number is unmoved

| Quantity | superseded record | this record |
|---|---|---|
| onset at `Vout` = 1.764 V | 62.01 … 93.80 mA | **62.01 … 93.83 mA** |
| onset into a dead short | 62.59 … 95.07 mA | **62.59 … 95.10 mA** |
| flatness, 0 V vs 1.5 V | +0.32 … +2.30 % | **+0.32 … +2.30 %** |
| `Vout` at a 50 mA resistive load | 1.7991 … 1.7996 V | **1.7989 … 1.7995 V** |
| sense-domain margin at 50 mA | ≥ 18.8 % | **≥ 18.75 %** |
| dissipation into a short, worst corner | 345.1 mW | **345.2 mW** |
| threshold at tt/27 °C/3.30 V | 74.92 mA | **74.92 mA** |
| corners outside the ratified 65–80 mA | 36 / 63 | **36 / 63** |

The largest per-corner change in any limit column across all 63 points is
**0.034 %**. That is the intended result and is the reason this record exists:
`Mclamp_bg` is off whenever the limit is idle, and when the limit engages it
changes *what the clamp has to overcome* (it releases the buffer's follower)
without changing *where the clamp trips*, which is set by
`41 · VREF · (Rref/Rbias) / Rsns` and by nothing in the output stage.

- **Sub-claim 1 — never engages inside the rated 0–50 mA load: PASS at all 63
  corners.** Worst-case onset **62.01 mA** at ss/−40 °C/2.97 V, +24.0 % above
  the rated load. The runner's own check reports *"corners where the limit
  engages at or below 50 mA: none"*.
- **Sub-claim 2 — constant-current, not foldback: PASS at all 63 corners.**
  Between `Vout` = 1.5 V and a dead short the current moves **+0.32 % to
  +2.30 %**, always upward.
- **Sub-claim 3 — survives a continuous short at Vin_max: PASS electrically,
  FAIL against the Thermal row's 290 mW.** 345.2 mW at ff/125 °C/3.63 V.
  Unchanged, and unchanged in cause.
- **Sub-claim 4 — 65–80 mA over PVT: FAIL, 36/63 corners outside.** 62.01 …
  93.83 mA = 77.92 mA ±20.4 %. The attribution is unchanged and is still not
  this design's: the FET-skew corners that hold resistors typical land on top
  of tt (fs 73.85 … 75.67, sf 73.99 … 75.65 against tt 73.91 … 75.63), while
  the resistor-only corners reproduce the ff/ss extremes to within 0.2 mA
  (res_ff 91.33 … 93.72 vs ff 91.45 … 93.83; res_ss 62.08 … 63.41 vs ss
  62.01 … 63.38). See `spec/decision-records/DR-0005-current-limit-window.md`.

**Overall: PASS on the safety-critical and behavioural halves of the row,
FAIL on the ±10 % window and on the Thermal row's 290 mW consequence — the
same verdict as the superseded record, with no number moved by more than
0.034 %.**

### One thing this bench cannot see, stated so it is not read as absent

This is a **DC** bench. `sim/enable-shutdown/records/20260802-023434-5ea33e8.md`
measures the limit clamp's gate in the *transient*, settled at 50 mA, and
reports it dipping below `VIN` − 0.2 V at 53 of 63 corners — the limit clamp
being modulated by a ~250 kHz settled-state limit cycle in the amplifier's own
output stage. That is not this row's onset claim (which is what "never engages
for `I_load` ≤ 50 mA" is written in terms of, and which passes here at every
corner), and the same record shows it is not clamp-driven: disconnecting both
clamps from `PASS_GATE` entirely makes the ring worse. It belongs to issue #53.
