The 50mA condition the ratified row also names is not automated in this deck -- see tb_psrr.spice's header comment for why (a real, documented ngspice limitation hit during implementation), left as a follow-up rather than fabricated. This record supersedes 20260801-002904-b64d60e -- a stale record from an earlier design state (commit b64d60e, before #39's current-limit/shutdown path and #44's soft-start landed) whose own stored per-corner values are physically sane; the append-only rule means that record is not itself in error and is left untouched. Re-running this SAME testbench topology against the CURRENT (post-#39/#44) ldo_core.spice with a cold, all-zero-guess Newton-Raphson start does not reproduce those sane numbers: #40's investigation found the .ac linearized around a non-physical operating point at most PVT points -- root-caused in #40 and fixed here. The deck's ideal 1 mA current sink had a second, unphysical DC solution in which the pass device is off and the sink holds VOUT tens of volts BELOW ground, drawing its current through forward-biased substrate junctions in the PDK device models; a cold Newton-Raphson start reached that solution at most corners, and everything the .ac then reported was a small-signal expansion about it. The sink is now compliance-limited (see tb_psrr.spice and sim/harness/README.md, 'Compliance-limited load sinks'): it draws its commanded current for VOUT well above ground and nothing at or below ground, which is what any real load does, and which deletes the unphysical solution rather than biasing the solver away from it. At the reported operating point the sink is an ideal current sink to within 1.3e-14 relative error and presents under 5.1e-16 S of small-signal conductance at 1 mA -- an open circuit at AC, exactly like the ideal sink it replaces -- and dc_vout_v / dc_iload_ma below read the .op plot so the operating point this PSRR is expanded about is reported and checked at every corner. That .op is reporting only, not steering: ngspice's ac command solves its own operating point, and the fix was confirmed order-independent by hand at tt_27c_3.30v -- running ac cold with no preceding op gives PSRR@1kHz = -25.2 dB with the old ideal sink (an expansion about the sub-ground root) and +77.91 dB with this sink, the latter identical to the op-then-ac ordering. No ratified threshold was changed.
