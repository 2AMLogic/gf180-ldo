## Result

**Mechanism: at 50 mA the main loop has no phase margin at the slow/cold corners of the DUT, and the transient "excursion" is the first trough of a large-signal oscillation. The pass-device width of DR-0034 moved it there; the DR-0037 servo is not the cause. The bench's 0.33 uF / 100 mOhm output network is outside the ratified Stability envelope (DR-0018: C_eff = 1 uF nominal, ESR >= 200 mOhm), and inside that envelope every point passes with >= 3x margin.**

The old DUT settles to 1.7995 V; the current DUT never does at `ss_-40c_2.97v` (a +-300 mV oscillation still present 80 us after the step). The 233 mV "undershoot" is the first trough of that oscillation, and the 476 mV "overshoot" at `sf_-40c_3.30v` is the same oscillation, which at PM ~ 0 grows slowly (quiet at 60-90 us, +-390 mV by 130-150 us) so that the release at 150 us catches it near its crest. The shapes differ (fast vs slow growth) but the cause is shared: `sf_-40c_3.30v` is a different shape of the same marginally unstable loop, not a separate mechanism.

### 6. Outcome

Routed as a **spec question** (`loom-daemon operator-decision apply`, issue #483): the row as ratified (0.33 uF, ESR from 0) and the narrowed Stability envelope (DR-0018: 1 uF, ESR >= 200 mOhm), on which DR-0034's width choice was evidenced, are not both satisfiable by this DUT. No design fix was found that fits here (the only lever measured, pass width, reopens DR-0034's `< 200 mV` dropout stretch); no spec-change decision record is written by this record.
