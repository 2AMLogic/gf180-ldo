**Why:** issue #55 changed `design/error_amp.sch` (`BG` exported as a port),
`design/ldo_softstart.sch` (`Mclamp_bg_ss` added) and
`design/ldo_ilimit.sch` (`Mclamp_bg` added), so that a clamp *steers* #51's
class-AB gate buffer instead of fighting it. This is the experiment that owns
the ratified **Startup** row's overshoot and settling clauses.

**The superseded record `20260801-142159-38317d9` was taken at `38317d9`, i.e.
before #51 landed.** No `sim/startup` record was ever taken on the post-#51,
pre-#55 design, so this comparison is *pre-#51 vs post-#55* and it is the
useful one: it answers whether the fix puts the startup row back where it was
before the buffer took the pass-gate node away from the clamps.

There is **no startup overshoot at any of the 45 points** — the measurement is
negative at every one, i.e. `VOUT` never exceeds its final value on the way
up — and the worst-case margin actually improves (the `overshoot_min_mv`
spread narrows from 195 % to 21 %). Ramp timing reproduces the pre-#51 record
to within 0.5 % at every corner.

**The FAIL is entirely `tsettle_*_ms` > 3 ms, and it is pre-existing and
already spec'd.** It is the same 36 rows (of 90 checks) as the superseded
record, at the same corners, with the same values to within 0.5 %; it is the
soft-start ramp being slower than the ratified 3 ms settling window at the
slow end of its own resistor × capacitor corner spread, which is
`spec/decision-records/DR-0006`. Nothing in #55 moves it, in either direction.

**Reading this against `sim/enable-shutdown`'s `vout_max_en`.** That
experiment's 63-point run at the same commit reports `vout_max_en` above the
+2 % bound at 63/63 corners. The two are not in conflict: `vout_max_en` is
`MAX v(vout)` over the *entire* 101 µs … 8 ms enable window, so it also
captures a settled-state limit cycle that begins after the ramp completes
(at tt/27 °C/2.97 V its maximum lands at 5.73 ms, two milliseconds after
startup finished). This bench measures overshoot against the final value over
the startup event itself, which is what the ratified row is written in terms
of. The settled-state ring is attributed — with a measurement — in
