This record supersedes 20260801-002618-b64d60e -- a stale record from an earlier design state (commit b64d60e, before #39's current-limit/shutdown path and #44's soft-start landed) whose own stored per-corner values are physically sane; the append-only rule means that record is not itself in error and is left untouched. Re-running this SAME testbench topology against the CURRENT (post-#39/#44) ldo_core.spice with a cold, all-zero-guess Newton-Raphson start does not reproduce those sane numbers: #40's investigation found 0 of 45 PVT points converging to the physical solution, instead reporting 8.6-58.9 V of 'load regulation' -- root-caused in #40 and fixed here. The deck's ideal 50 mA current sink had a second, unphysical DC solution in which the pass device is off and the sink holds VOUT tens of volts BELOW ground, drawing its current through forward-biased substrate junctions in the PDK device models; a cold Newton-Raphson start reached that solution at most corners, and #40 showed a .nodeset hint alone could not clear it for a swept deck. The sink is now compliance-limited (see tb_load_reg.spice and sim/harness/README.md, 'Compliance-limited load sinks'): it draws its commanded current for VOUT well above ground and nothing at or below ground, which is what any real load does, and which deletes the unphysical solution rather than biasing the solver away from it. The sink is an ideal current sink to within 1.4e-14 relative error at every operating point reported here, and iload_1ma_ma/iload_50ma_ma below measure the actually-delivered current so that claim is checked at every corner instead of assumed. No ratified threshold was changed. This remains a DC/loop-closure measurement only: it does not account for stability (#10) or for the DR-0001 capacitor window.
