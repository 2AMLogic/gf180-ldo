  Verdict:
  - Vout near the 1.8 V target, enabled: **PASS** (1.79945 V, 0.031 % low).
    The superseded record measured 1.80242 V (0.13 % high) with the 70 dB
    placeholder amp; the real amplifier's 114 dB of DC gain shrinks the
    finite-gain error 4x, and its residual systematic offset (+366 uV at the
    feedback node, consistent with the 280-610 uV measured across PVT in
    `sim/amp-openloop/records/20260801-002830-712cb87.md`) sets the sign.
  - Placeholder -> `error_amp` swap is mechanical: **PASS** (the only change
    in `ldo_core.sch` is the instantiated symbol name; `error_amp.sym` reuses
    the placeholder's pin coordinates and the `INP INN OUT VDD VSS` pin
    order, so no net was rewired -- `python3 design/netlist.py --check`
    verifies the pinout invariants)
  - No rail-stuck nodes, enabled: **PASS** (Vout, FB and the loop node all
    sit at sane intermediate values, none pinned at VIN or VSS; the loop node
    at 2.547 V corresponds to Vsg = 0.753 V on the 2 mm pass device)
  - Enable stub turns the pass device off: **PASS** (Vout collapses to
    ~0.25 uV and the pass-device gate is pulled to within 0.4 mV of VIN by
    the enable clamp)
  - Disabled-state supply current: **9.24 uA** -- and this is the number to
    read carefully. The superseded record measured 3.3 uA disabled, because
    the placeholder amplifier drew no bias current at all. With a real,
    self-biased amplifier the disabled-state current is the amplifier's own
    bias branch, which `Men` does not gate: **the ratified "shutdown Iq
    < 3 uA" row cannot be met on this interface.** That is an interface
    finding for #11 (which owns shutdown characterization), not a defect in
    this record -- the 5-port swap-in contract has no enable pin, so closing
    that row needs either a gated supply for this cell or a renegotiated
    pinout. See `design/error_amp.md` S7.
  - **Overall: PASS** (against this record's stated claim -- loop closure and
    swap functionality -- with the shutdown-Iq finding above logged, not
    swept under the rug)
