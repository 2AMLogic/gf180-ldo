  Verdict, as a delta against the superseded record:

  - Enabled `Vout`: **1.799451 V — bit-identical** to the superseded record.
    The limit block is genuinely inert in regulation: its sense branch draws
    1/40 of the pass current and returns it to `VOUT`, so it neither loads
    the output nor shifts the operating point. **PASS**
  - Enabled pass-gate: 2.548565 V vs. 2.547041 V before, i.e. **+1.5 mV**.
    That is the extra `Vsg` the pass device needs to also supply the sense
    replica's share. **PASS** (no rail-stuck node; `Vsg` = 0.751 V)
  - Enabled supply current: 1.012916 mA vs. 1.010775 mA — **+2.1 µA**, the
    limit block's threshold bias plus comparator tail at this corner. The
    corner-swept version of this number (and its verdict against the < 30 µA
    row) is in the enable/shutdown record. **PASS**

    reduction**, and the point of this re-run. The superseded record
    concluded that *"the ratified shutdown Iq < 3 µA row cannot be met on
    this interface"*, because the amplifier had no enable pin and kept its
    bias branch alive. #11 appended `EN` to `error_amp` and gated the bias
    inside it, so the row is met — verified across the full PVT matrix, at
    its binding ff/125 °C/3.63 V corner, in the enable/shutdown record
    (0.203 µA there; this corner is simply a cold, typical, low-leakage one).
    **PASS**
  - Disabled output and pass gate: `Vout` = 0.27 µV, `PASS_GATE` pinned at
    `VIN` to within the print resolution. **PASS**
